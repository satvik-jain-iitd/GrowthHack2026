# Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I reach out for help on Build-vs-Buy framework?

For Build-vs-Buy related queries, please reach out to us on slack [#ea-build-vs-buy](https://aexp.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C08EJKNCX0U) or on email at [techbvb@aexp.com](mailto:techbvb@aexp.com). For portal specific issues, please post on [#arch-portal-help](https://my.slack.com/archives/C05655U6GMC) Slack channel with any questions or issues you might encounter.

### Does Build-vs-Buy framework apply to all requests? Is there a criteria?

Build-vs-Buy does not apply to all requests. The eligibility criteria are documented in the in the [Enterprise Architecture Build-vs-Buy Framework Guide](https://architecture1.aexp.com/docs/0f95a9e2-fd12-42f2-bec5-00e547097e43) under [When to use the Build-vs-Buy Framework?](https://architecture1.aexp.com/docs/0f95a9e2-fd12-42f2-bec5-00e547097e43#when-to-use-the-build-vs-buy-framework)

### Which Tech standard enforces the Build-vs-Buy framework?

The Build-vs-Buy framework is enforced and required by the [TECH05.10 Architecture as Design Standard](https://spaces.aexp.com/sites/global%20standards%20library/Shared%20Documents/TECH05.10%20Architecture%20as%20Design%20Standard.pdf)

### What is the SLA for the E2E process for BvB to complete, if all information is available?  Also, if there is a need to do urgent onboarding (for legitimate business needs) is there a path to accelerate the process?

The Build-vs-Buy framework is laid out as a Self-Service process/framework that teams need to execute with EA’s help. Teams will need to perform the assessment and EA will help and guide on the process. Please work with the assigned EA Architect for your company domain.

### Is GSM aware of this new framework/process? As of today, BU/UCIO Tech Teams are reaching out to GSM team for submitting procurement requests (in SmartBuy), so it's crucial that GSM is brought in on this process.

We worked very closely with our GSM partners on this process. In fact, GSM team – James Houghton (B45 – Source-to-Contract) and Bill Anderson (Director -Purchasing) co-presented the TechChat along with EA.

### How many teams have gone through this process so far? What has been their feedback?

We’ve had few teams already initiated the process with few having completed as well. We have received feedback from teams on the dimensions and questionnaire in the Build-vs-Buy scorecard. We are currently updating those and we are constantly looking for feedback on anything that helps to improve this process.

### Apart from keeping a record (ADR) of the Build-vs-Buy decision, is there intended to be a bias toward buying vs. building?

There is no bias toward buy or build or re-use or partner. The BvB assessment should be done to objectively determine the best solution available. However, the record-keeping is surely an important aspect of the process to be able to demonstrate the due diligence done in making the decision.

### How do we determine when to build and when to buy (or partner or re-use)?

The BvB assessment should be conducted objectively across all the listed dimensions defined by the process. If the expertise is really with an external product, that would show up in the assessment. The assessment criteria is not limited to only domain expertise. Several other factors (for example, total cost of ownership) may contribute to the ultimate decision.

### Do the BU Teams or the UCIO Tech Teams need to raise the Build-vs-Buy Request?

The onboarding request may be raised either by the business or the tech team. Please refer to the various engagements paths as listed in the [Enterprise Architecture Build-vs-Buy Framework Guide](https://architecture1.aexp.com/docs/0f95a9e2-fd12-42f2-bec5-00e547097e43)

### Is there a flow or RACI that indicates the responsibilities of all the teams involved in this process for the various intake paths?

Yes, there are detailed swimlane diagrams for each of the intake paths and are documented in the [Enterprise Architecture Build-vs-Buy Framework Guide](https://architecture1.aexp.com/docs/0f95a9e2-fd12-42f2-bec5-00e547097e43)

### Can I add more dimensions and questions to the available scorecard specific to my use case? Is that allowed?

Yes, the BvB scorecard is a customizable artifact and it is in fact recommended that teams configure it so that the most meaningful dimensions are brought in for the comparative analysis. The dimensions and questions for the BvB assessment should be reviewed with the assigned EA Architect to ensure all relevant aspects are covered including mandatory/optional sections. If you feel there are generic dimensions and questions that would add value to the generic scorecard, please let the EA Architect know and we will add them to the scorecard template for broader use by the enterprise.

### Does the Build-vs-Buy framework apply to existing software that is already in use? Does the framework apply to these software when they are up for renewals?

At this time (as of Jul-2025), Build-vs-Buy only applies to new applications/initiatives/procurement requests or a significant new technology change to an existing application. Build-vs-Buy diligence is not required for renewals on existing software.

### Should I plan for additional effort on the Build-vs-Buy process?

When building software internally or choosing vendor software, this tech diligence of comparing all available options is required to be done as a good practice and EA's Build-vs-Buy framework makes it a standardized consistent process to follow with a well-defined set of artifacts to support the decision like the BvB Scorecard and the Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

### Does the Build-vs-Buy ADR need to be presented on the Architecture Review Board (ARB)? Also, should that be the Enterprise ARB (EARB) or Domain ARB (DARB)?

Yes, in most cases the Build-vs-Buy ADR needs to be presented on ARB. If the ADR is enterprise impacting, it would be EARB, else DARB. Please work with your assigned EA Architect to determine which ARB it needs to be presented on.
