# Billing Portal

The Resy Billing Portal can be found at [billing.resy.com](http://billing.resy.com/). This is where you can view and manage invoices, update payment methods, and more.

**Please note:**

* Login credentials for [billing.resy.com](https://billing.resy.com/) are unrelated to ResyOS.
* Partners no longer have the option to create their own portal account.

### **In this article:**

* Welcome to the new Resy Billing Portal dashboard
* New User Billing Portal Access & Account Details
* View and Pay Invoices
* Setting up Autopay

---

  

**Please note:** If you have not received an email with your new username and a one-time link for setting up your password, please reach out to [billing@resy.com](mailto:billing@resy.com) for portal access.

## **Welcome to the new Resy Billing Portal dashboard**

On the main dashboard, you have visibility into your invoices, payments, subscriptions, user settings, payments methods, and more.

1. On clicking your name at the top right corner of the screen, you can view your active accounts and manage invoices and payments by clicking into them individually.
	1. If you have multiple accounts, you will now be able to toggle between your accounts from your dashboard by clicking the dropdown. If you do not see your other accounts, please reach out to [billing@resy.com](mailto:billing@resy.com).
2. The “Invoices” tab at the top menu bar opens details of your active invoices, the balance pending, and due date.
	1. Please know that invoices and debit memos from our prior billing portal will not be viewable in the new billing portal.
3. The “Payments” tab at the top menu bar allows you to make payments for outstanding balances and different invoices.
	1. You can select “Outstanding Balance” to pay the remaining total balance on your account.
	2. You can select an invoice to pay from a list of open invoices to pay.
	3. This is where you manage and update your payment methods, set a default one, and add a new one as needed.
	4. You can view your payment history here.
	5. Changes made here will be reflected on the main dashboard.
4. The “Subscriptions” button at the top menu bar opens your active subscriptions with the term start and end dates and activity status.

## **New User Billing Portal Access & Account Details**

**To manage Account Details and update information:**

1. Click on “Settings” at the top right corner.
2. Under the “Account Details” tab, you can click on “Update Information” to change.  
[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img03]]

**Adding a New user to the Portal:**

1. While logged in, Navigate to Settings in the upper right corner of the page.  
[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img04]]
2. Click “Account Information”.  
[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img05]]
3. Click the “Contacts” tab, then “Add a Contact”.  
[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img06]]
4. Fill out the information for the new contact and hit “Submit”.  
[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img07]]

The user has now been added to your account and will recieve an email from [billing@resy.com](mailto:billing@resy.com) with an invitation link.

When the newly added user clicks that one time link to accept the invitation, they can set up their password for their account and will then be able to log in and access your billing information.

If the user gets an error that the invitation has already been used, then their account exists in the system, and they have two options:

1. Reset their password from the main portal page and follow the instructions sent in the email.  
[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img08]]
2. Create new Account” with an email address that matches the invitation.

## **View and Pay Invoices**

* **Invoices**
	1. Click "Invoices" button from the top menu bar.
	2. Here you can view invoice details, including your outstanding balance and due date.

[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img09]]  

* **Payment Management**

          Click the “Payments” button from the top menu bar.

**To make a payment:**

1. Navigate to the “Make a Payment” tab.
2. Select the “Outstanding Balance” to pay on your account as well as the invoice. from a dropdown of the open ones.
3. Click “Next” after you’ve made the selections to move to Step 2.
4. Review and Confirm on the following screen to move to Step 3.
5. The following screen (Step 3) should populate a payment confirmation.

[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img10]]

[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img11]]  

**To update payment methods:**

* Click “Manage Payment Method” to add a new payment method on file (ACH, Credit Card) and set it to default.

[[IMAGE: resy-billing-portal__img12]]

  

**To view payment history:**

1. Click on the “Payment History” tab to find your entire payment history, including the payment date, amount, and payment status.
