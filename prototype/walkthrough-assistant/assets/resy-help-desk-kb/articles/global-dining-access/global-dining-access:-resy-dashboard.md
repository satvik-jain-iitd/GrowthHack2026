# How do I use the Resy Dashboard? (For Global Dining Access partners)

Welcome to Global Dining Access by Resy! This article will give you an overview of the dashboard tools available to manage your account. To access the Resy Dashboard, click [here](https://os.resy.com/portal).

When you log in, the dashboard will open to your homepage. To navigate the dashboard tools, click through the options on the left-hand menu bar. Below are highlights of the most important tools for managing your Global Dining Access settings.

* **Reports:** Access reporting for daily reservations, monthly covers, guest feedback, and monthly guestbook
* **Guests:**Access your Exclusive Groups; this is the primary tool you’ll use to manage your new Resy Global Access Reservations and transfer the Card Member reservation details to your reservation books
* **Servic****e:**View/edit your table availability, make single day edits to your tables, & take reservations offline for blackout dates
* **Venue:**Access your restaurant profile information, images, and online content
* **Accounts:**Access User Permissions; manage your existing logins and add new team members for access to the Resy Dashboard and email alerts
