# Global Dining Access Partners: Receive and Transfer Global Dining Access Reservations to My Books

This article will walk you through how to manage your incoming Global Dining Access reservations and transfer the reservation details to your reservation books. Acknowledging a reservation as "Mark as Transferred" lets American Express know you have all the details you need to facilitate the Card Member reservation.**We ask that you acknowledge and transfer all reservations within 24 hours of the booking time.**

# Resy Email Alerts

Once your exclusive American Express Card Member inventory is live, new Global Dining Access (GDA) reservations are confirmed within 24-hours by following the steps below:

1. Open the new reservation alert email from Resy.
2. Enter booking information (name, contact info, time, date, party size, etc.) in your primary reservation system.
3. Select “Mark as Transferred” in the email alert.

To manage your new reservations from the Resy Dashboard, click the “Manage Resy Global Access Reservations” link included in the email alert.

# Using the Resy Dashboard to Manage Your Reservations

The **“Exclusive Groups”** section of the Resy Dashboard can be used to manage all aspects of your upcoming reservations. Here you can view all your reservations that are “On the Books”, mark reservations as “Transferred”, make reservation changes, or facilitate cancellations.  

To get started, click **"Guests"**from the left-hand menu options, then click **"Exclusive Groups"**.

[[IMAGE: global-dining-access:-new-reservation-notification-and-transfers__img02]]  

Scroll to the “Global Dining Access” section to see all the new reservations that need to be transferred to your reservation books. Here you can view the reservation details and click the ✓ icon to “Mark as Transferred” when you've added the details to your reservation books. **Please acknowledge and transfer all reservations to your reservation books within 24 hours of the booking time.**

[[IMAGE: global-dining-access:-new-reservation-notification-and-transfers__img03]]

Click “Show Transferred Reservations” to expand the view and show all your reservations. This is useful if you need to review any reservation details that are already on the books.
