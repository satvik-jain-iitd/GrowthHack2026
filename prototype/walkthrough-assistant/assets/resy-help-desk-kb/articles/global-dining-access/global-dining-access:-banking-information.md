# Global Dining Access: How do I add Banking Info to my account?

This article will inform you how to securely attach a bank account in your Resy Dashboard settings to facilitate the direct deposit of funds collected through Resy for prepaid reservations and cancellation fees.

1. [Go to the ResyOS Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal).
2. From the left-hand menu options, choose **“Venue”**.
3. Select **“Transaction Settings”** from the drop-down menu.  
[[IMAGE: global-dining-access:-banking-information__img01]]
4. Click **“Banking Info”**.
5. Fill out **all**sections in the form for the banking information you want funds directly deposited to.
	* **Please note**: When filling out the information, **Locality**= City and **Region**= State initials.
6. Click “Save”.  
[[IMAGE: global-dining-access:-banking-information__img02]]
