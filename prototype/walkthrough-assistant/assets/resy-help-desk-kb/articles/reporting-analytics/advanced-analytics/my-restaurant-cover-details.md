# Advanced Analytics - My Restaurant: Cover Details

The **My Restaurant: Cover Details** report is a bird’s eye view of your restaurant and perfect to bring along to meetings with management and owners. 

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-cover-details__img01]]

  

The filters are defaulted to show your data from the last 30 days and all service periods. To look at a specific date range or shift, click into each filter and set. You can also filter for First Time Diners.  

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-cover-details__img02]]

**Reservation Source (Grouped)** lets you filter by whatever channel your guests are booking through.  

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-cover-details__img03]]

* RESERVATION – IN HOUSE: Reservations made by your team on an iPad or through the dashboard.
* RESERVATION – ONLINE: Reservations made by guests on your website, on Resy.com or the Resy app, and through Google or Instagram integration.
* WALKIN: All guests who have walked into your restaurant without booking in advance.

  

**Top Cover Days (All Time)** shows the days your restaurant did the most business. We typically see Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day, and NYE topping the charts.  

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-cover-details__img04]]

 

**No-Show Rate** shows the average percent of reservations that no- showed vs dined. Industry standard can range anywhere from 5-20% and typically increases around the holidays. If you have a high no-show rate, you might consider implementing a [cancellation policy](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-add-cancellation-policy-to-a-reservation-BkF2ZDQIu), [double confirmation message](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/add-a-pop-up-message-to-your-guest-booking-experience-By3SRkDyK), and/or an additional [follow-up confirmation text](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/standard-resy-automated-sms-content-S110vQIu).  

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-cover-details__img05]]

 

**Late Cancelled Reservations** shows what percentage of your cancelled reservations are cancelled within and outside of 24-hours. We consider a reservation a Late Cancel if it is cancelled within 24-hours of the reservation time. If you have a high percentage of Late Cancels, consider revising your text message timing to give guests more time to cancel in advance. 

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-cover-details__img06]]

 

**Covers by Day of Week** shows your total cover count every day of the week for the last five weeks.  

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-cover-details__img07]]

  

**Covers by Source** shows what channels your guests are booking through. There’s usually little variance month over month so you may want to review this for a longer date range. 

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-cover-details__img08]]

| We recommend reviewing this report monthly with your managers and business leaders. |
