# Advanced Analytics - Today: Spread of Shift

**The Today: Spread of Shift** report helps to visualize today’s upcoming service by covers and reservations. We recommend sharing this with Back of House (BOH) so they can better anticipate large parties and high-volume times. 

[[IMAGE: today-spread-of-shift__img01]]

  

  

The filters are defaulted to show your data from all day today. To look at a specific shift, day, time, or party size, click into each filter and set. 

[[IMAGE: today-spread-of-shift__img02]]

 

On the top, you’ll see your Spread of Shift by **covers**. Looking at your shift by covers helps you identify potential opportunities to push the books.  

[[IMAGE: today-spread-of-shift__img03]]

On the bottom, you’ll see your Spread of Shift by **reservations**. Looking at your shift by reservations helps you plan your server sections and prepare your kitchen team for service.  

[[IMAGE: today-spread-of-shift__img04]]

| We recommend reviewing this report daily with your floor managers and BOH. |
