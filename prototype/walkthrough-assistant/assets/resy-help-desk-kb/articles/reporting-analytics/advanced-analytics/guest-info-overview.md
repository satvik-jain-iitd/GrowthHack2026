# Advanced Analytics - Guest Info: Overview

The **Guest Info: Overview** report highlights guests of note and provides insight into where your guests are coming from. 

[[IMAGE: guest-info-overview__img01]]

  

The filters are defaulted to show your data from the last 30 days and all service periods. To look at a specific date range or shift, click into each filter and set. You can also filter for Global Dining Access (GDA) Diners.  

[[IMAGE: guest-info-overview__img02]]

  

**Guests by Origin** is a breakdown of where your guests are visiting you from. This restaurant is in New York and with over 75% of their guests being local, they might focus their marketing budget in the neighborhood.  

[[IMAGE: guest-info-overview__img03]]

  

Resy defines **New Regulars**as guests who have visited you for a third time. We recommend tagging these guests as regulars in your system so you can introduce yourself on their next visit! 

[[IMAGE: guest-info-overview__img04]]

  

The **Guest Turn Time** reports highlight your guests with the shortest and longest turn times. We recommend tagging these guests as such so you can adjust the turn time on their next reservation. 

[[IMAGE: guest-info-overview__img05]]

| We recommend reviewing this report monthly with your managers and door team. |
