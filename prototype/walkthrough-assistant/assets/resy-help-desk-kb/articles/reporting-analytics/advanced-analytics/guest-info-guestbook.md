# Advanced Analytics - Guest Info: Guestbook

---

  

The **Guest Info: Guestbook** is your restaurant’s full guestbook. 

[[IMAGE: guest-info-guestbook__img01]]

 

The filters are defaulted to show your complete guestbook. To look at a specific date range, click into Service Period Date and set a range.  

[[IMAGE: guest-info-guestbook__img02]]

**Completed Reservations** >=1 shows all guests who have dined with you at least once. If you’d like to include guests who have been on your notify list but never dined set this to 0.  

The **Is Marketable (Yes / No)*** field is defaulted to Yes and indicates if the guest has opted-in to receiving marketing communications from your restaurant.  

To sort for guests based on **Tags**, click into that field and start typing whatever tag you’re interested in or click the dropdown carrot to view all the tags in your system.  

To sort **VIPs**, select Yes to see only VIPs and select No to see guests without VIP status.
