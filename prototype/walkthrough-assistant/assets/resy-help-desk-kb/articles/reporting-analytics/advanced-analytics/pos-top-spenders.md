# Advanced Analytics - POS: Top Spenders

| Please Note: Advanced Analytics reporting is available for Platform 360 restaurant partners. If you are interested in learning more or upgrading, please reach out to your account manager or [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com). |
| --- |

  

The **POS: Top Spenders** report lets you sort your top spenders by total spend, reservation count, Per Person Average (PPA), average party size, and spend per visit. Use this to tag guests in your system. 

[[IMAGE: pos-top-spenders__img01]] 

The filters are defaulted to show your data from the last 30 days, all shifts and all-party sizes. To look at a specific date range, shift, or party size, click into each filter and set. You can also filter by Completed Reservations, First Time Diners, and Global Dining Access (GDA) Diners. 

[[IMAGE: pos-top-spenders__img02]]
