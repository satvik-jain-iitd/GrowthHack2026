# Advanced Analytics - Guest Info: Details

The **Guest Info: Details** report highlights guests with the most visits, your VIPs, and No-Show Diners. Use this report to tag guests in your system.  

[[IMAGE: guest-info-details__img01]]

 

The filters are defaulted to show your data from the last 30 days, all shifts and all reservations. To look at a specific date range or shift, click into each filter and set. You can also filter by No-Show, Cancelled, or Completed reservations. 

[[IMAGE: guest-info-details__img02]]

  

Use **Visit Counts** to see which of your guests dine with you the most and their respective cancelled reservation rate. A high visit count may earn your guest a VIP or Regular tag, a high cancellation rate may warrant a note to your door team to double confirm those reservations. 

[[IMAGE: guest-info-details__img03]]

  

**No-Show Diners** shows which of your guests no-show the most. Consider tagging these guests so your team can double confirm their reservations ahead of service. 

[[IMAGE: guest-info-details__img04]]

 

**VIPs** shows you which of your VIPs have joined you recently and who is on the books for tonight! 

[[IMAGE: guest-info-details__img05]]

| We recommend you review this report monthly with reservationists and managers. |
