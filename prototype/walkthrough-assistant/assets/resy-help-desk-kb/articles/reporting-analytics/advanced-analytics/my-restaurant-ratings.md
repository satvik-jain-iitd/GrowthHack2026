# Advanced Analytics - My Restaurant: Ratings

The **My Restaurant: Ratings** report illustrates the ratings your restaurant has received. Use this report to spot trends in your reviews and share with your team. 

[[IMAGE: my-restaurant-ratings__img01]]

 

The filters are defaulted to show your data from the last 30 days and all service periods. To look at a specific date range or shift, click into each filter and set. 

Resy ratings are a closed- loop system, meaning only restaurants can see the review and individual star rating. After each visit, guests receive an email from Resy asking them to rate their experience on a 5-star scale. Guests have the option to include a comment and choose factors driving their review such as Food, Service, Vibe, and Other. 

| We recommend reviewing this report monthly with managers and staff. |
