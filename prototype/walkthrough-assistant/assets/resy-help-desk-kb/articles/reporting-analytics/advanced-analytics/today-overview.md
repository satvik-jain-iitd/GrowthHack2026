# Advanced Analytics - Today:Overview

The Today Overview report is a handy reference designed for pre-shift review with your floor managers. 

[[IMAGE: today-overview__img01]]

  

The filter is defaulted to show your data from all day today. To look at a specific shift, click into the Service Period filter and select your shift. 

[[IMAGE: today-overview__img02]]

Across the top, you’ll find (from left to right): 

* Your final cover count from this day last year
* How many covers are on the books tonight
* Your projected cover count by the end of the shift

The projected cover count looks back at that day of the week for the last five weeks, adds your average walk-in traffic, and subtracts your average no-shows to predict your final cover count for the shift. 

[[IMAGE: today-overview__img03]]

  

On the left, is a pie chart of your reservations’ statuses: 

* Left Message
* Confirmed
* Unconfirmed

If you notice a good portion of reservations are unconfirmed, you may want to manually reach out to those reservations. Using [Quick Replies](https://helpdesk.resy.com/text-message-quick-replies-SywMGDm8d), you can send the same message to guests on your Booked list more easily. First, start by creating a saved reply in the Settings on your iPad and then, in the Chat tab on the guest profile,  tap on the blue bubble with the lightning bolt to bring up that canned message.

[[IMAGE: today-overview__img04]]

  

On the right, we count:  

* First Time Guests who are on the books
* Number of guests on your Notify list
* VIPs

We recommend highlighting those VIPs on your Spread of Shift report for the kitchen.  

[[IMAGE: today-overview__img05]]
