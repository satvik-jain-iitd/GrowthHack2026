# Advanced Analytics - POS: Per Person Average (PPA)

| Please Note: Advanced Analytics reporting is available for Platform 360 restaurant partners. If you are interested in learning more or upgrading, please reach out to your account manager or [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com). |
| --- |

The **POS: PPA** report shows your Per Person Average (PPA) by shift and reservation source. Use this to fine- tune your books. 

[[IMAGE: pos-per-person-average-ppa__img01]]

  

The filters are defaulted to show your data from the last 30 days. To look at a specific shift or date range, click into the filter and set. You can also filter for First Time Diners and Global Dining Access (GDA) Diners.  

[[IMAGE: pos-per-person-average-ppa__img02]]

  

Across the top, you’ll find your PPA for each shift. If your restaurant is not open for a certain shift, you won’t see any information in that shift’s tile.  

[[IMAGE: pos-per-person-average-ppa__img03]]

 

**PPA by Reservation Source** shows how much your guests spend depending on what channel they booked through. Typically, walk-ins spend the least and reservations booked by your in-house team spend the most. This might lead you to update your table access on your shifts to offer more reservations online and allocate fewer tables for walk-ins. 

[[IMAGE: pos-per-person-average-ppa__img04]]

  

**Shift PPA by Source** shows how much your guests spend depending on what channel they booked through by shift. Review this when you’re setting up your books to maximize revenue -– if walk-ins spend the least, consider allocating fewer tables for walk-ins and more for in-house bookings. 

                         [[IMAGE: pos-per-person-average-ppa__img05]][[IMAGE: pos-per-person-average-ppa__img06]]
