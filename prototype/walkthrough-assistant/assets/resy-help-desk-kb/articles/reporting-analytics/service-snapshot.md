# Service Snapshot

The Service Snapshot are reports that give you an extensive overview of your current and previous day covers. It also provides projections and data-based forecasts to give you and your team more insight into how your service will run.  

**Data Reported for the Previous Day:**

* The **Cover Source** pie chart showing where your guests are booking their reservations.
* **New Regulars** shows you any diners who joined you for the third time last night.
* The **Average Turn Times by Party Size by Server**report shows server performance based on the party size.
* Your **Spread of Shift** shows your actual pacing from the previous day/night.
* The **Competitive Performance** section first stacks you against yourself, showing completed covers that day last year vs total covers yesterday and the percentage.
* The **Market Performance Last 30 Days** shows your cover trends against the other Resy restaurants in your market.

**Data Reported for the Current Day:**

* The **Daily Dashboard** provides a snapshot of the upcoming service including predicted covers and various guest breakdown.
* **Average Performance from Past 5 Weeks by Day of Week** shows your average total covers, day-of covers added, walk-ins, cancellations, and no-shows for this day (Mondays, Thursdays, etc.) over the last five weeks.
* Your **Spread of Shift** shows your pacing for the night, where you can expect the pops in volume and maybe where you can take a few more walk-ins.
* **Today’s Reservation Status** clues you into how many of your reservations have confirmed via the automated confirmation text.

**Please note:** Same-day bookings are automatically confirmed.  

* More guest breakdown shows you how many guests are on the Notify list, as well as how many first-time diners and VIPs are on the books.

**How to Receive These Reports**

To receive these reports to your email address, sign up for them in the Resy OS Dashboard:

1. Click on "Accounts" from the left-hand menu.
2. Click "Users".
3. Select the three dots next to your user profile.
4. Click "Edit".
5. Select the "Reports" tab.
6. Select the check box next to "Service Snapshot".
7. Click "Update User".

These reports are sent to your inbox at 6:00 am local time.
