Features included with POS Integration

* Real-time check level data in the reservation profile under the "Check" tab.
* Automated status updates to "check dropped" in Resy OS when you update a status to “check closed” in POS.
* POS data reporting.
* Real time course status updates. Reservations statuses in Resy OS will automatically update when the corresponding course: appetizer, entrée, and dessert is selected in your POS. More information can be found [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/pos-integration-automatic-course-status-BkqVQL0n).

#### Check Matching is based on these three criteria:

1. Matching table numbers (exactly) between the POS ticket and Resy reservation.
2. Matching guest count between the POS ticket and Resy reservation.

For party sizes of 6+, there is now a buffer of +/- 2 guests to allow check matching. For example, if a party size is 8, look to match checks with cover counts of 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.

      3. The time the reservation is seated in Resy is within 15 minutes of the time the ticket is opened in the POS.

## Reporting

Leveraging POS and reservation data unlocks insights about guest behavior, sales forecasting and more. Being able to read and understand these reports will help you make more informed decisions about your business. [Learn more about Resy's POS Reports](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/resy-os-pos-reporting-SJRv9oy2O). 

You can add users to have **access**to the Automated POS Reports which are generated and sent daily and weekly. Check out the instructions on [how to update a user’s permissions to add them to the distribution list for this report](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/user-permissions-r1zCWDmU_).

[[IMAGE: pos-integrations-BJ4QMDQLd__img02]]

With the POS Reports permission toggled On, you’ll receive the following reports to help utilize your POS and Resy data:

* Daily Forecast. (**Please note:** this report is only sent for dinner shifts.)
* Trends Dashboard sent weekly.
* POS Overview sent weekly.
