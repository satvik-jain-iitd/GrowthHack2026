We’ve compiled a list of customizations along with examples of how some Resy partners are making the most out of Platform 360. ## Platform 360 Customizations

  

### **Email and Text Messages to Guests**

Have anything important you want guests to know after booking? Share information about parking, dress code, grace periods, or adjust the timing of text messages. Learn more about customizing guest emails [here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/customizing-email-content-H1u6bDQ8d) and text messages [here.](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/customizing-text-message-content-HJP6WwQLO)

### **Table Statuses**

You can add a bussed table icon or change the color of the seated icon to match your previous system. Contact your Implementation Specialist and adjust table statuses before going live. The Support team or your dedicated Restaurant Success Manager can set this up once you’re live on Resy.

### **Reporting & Insights**

Get access to see key insights on your restaurant such as the percentage of bookings coming from your website, Google, or Resy or which guests have dined more than 5+ times. Your Implementation Specialist can get custom reports set up before going live. The Support team or your dedicated Restaurant Success Manager can set this up once you’re live on Resy.

Example:

### **Post-Meal Surveys**

Each guest that has an email on file after dining will receive a [post-dining survey](http://helpdesk.resy.com/en/articles/3020424-resy-surveys). You can set up your survey with our standard questions or customize surveys by filling out the following [form](https://partnerfeedback.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_0rIs8hSFNjpLZ78).

## Platform 360 Features

  

### **Third-Party Payment**

Third-Party Payment allows a third party to purchase the full meal or an item (for example, wine bottle) for an existing reservation. The guest will receive a PCI compliant credit card authorization form via text message, and you'll be able to charge third-party guests directly through Resy OS. More information can be found [here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-use-resy-prepay-feature-in-resyos-rJTzMvXLu).

Your Implementation Specialist can set up third-party payment before going live, or you can reach out to Resy Support for assistance once you’re live on Resy.

### Dashboard Booking

You can book reservations directly on the Resy Dashboard, allowing your reservationist to provide a better guest experience and toggle between different venues more efficiently. In addition, the booking process was built to be as flexible as your reservationist, so the steps to book a reservation can be completed in any order they see fit. More information can be found [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/desktop-reservation-flow-SJEDlJNn2). 

### **Exclusive Groups**

Reward your best guests by allocating online reservations to a select group, such as VIPs, regulars, and concierges. VIP, concierge, or guest tags can be used to create an online channel. These groups can be applied to your tables in the availability section of your shift settings. Information on how to use Exclusive Groups can be found [here](http://helpdesk.resy.com/en/articles/3188472-how-to-use-exclusive-online-booking-channels).

## Plan Change Options

  

### Upgrade

If you're interested in upgrading your plan, please reach out to our Support Team at [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com).

* Upgrade from a higher tier package (i.e., Platform to Platform 360).
* Upgrade contract terms (i.e., monthly to yearly).
* POS Integration: To use a POS Integration, you will need to upgrade to Platform, or Platform 360.

### Downgrade

If you'd like to downgrade your plan, please reach out to our Support Team at [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com).

* Downgrade to a lower tier package.

### Contract Changes

* For contract changes, please reach out to your Restaurant Success Manager or the Support team.
