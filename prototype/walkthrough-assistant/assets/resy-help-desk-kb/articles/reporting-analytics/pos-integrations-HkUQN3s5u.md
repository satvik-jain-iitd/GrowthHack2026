### Features included with POS Integration

* Real-time check level data in the reservation profile under the “Check” tab
* The “Check” tab shows if an item is “On Hold”. Each time the check tab is closed and reopened, that information will refresh
* Industry leading POS reporting for forecasting, sales trends, and guest insights
* “Check Closed” in your POS updates the status to “Check Dropped” status automatically in ResyOS
* Real time course status updates. Reservations statuses in Resy OS will automatically update when the corresponding course: appetizer, entrée, and dessert is selected in your POS or when a check is closed in your POS system. More information can be found [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/pos-integration-automatic-course-status-BkqVQL0n)

#### Check Matching is based on these three criteria:

1. Matching table numbers (exactly) between the POS ticket and Resy reservation.
2. Matching guest count between the POS ticket and Resy reservation.
3. The time the reservation is seated in Resy is within 15 minutes of the time the ticket is opened in the POS.

### **Reporting**

Leveraging POS and reservation data unlocks insights about guest behavior, sales forecasting, and more. Being able to read and understand these reports will help you make more informed decisions about your business.

You can give users access to the Automated POS Reports which are generated and sent daily and weekly. [Click here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/user-permissions-r1zCWDmU_) for instructions on how to update a user’s permissions to add them to the distribution list for this report.

  

[[IMAGE: pos-integrations-HkUQN3s5u__img02]]

With the POS Reports permission toggled 'On', you’ll receive the following reports to help utilize your POS and Resy data:

* Daily Forecast, sent daily and **only**for dinner shifts
* Trends Dashboard sent weekly
* POS Overview sent weekly

Learn more about Resy's POS Reports [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/resy-os-pos-reporting-SJRv9oy2O). 

If you're interested in learning more or upgrading, please reach out to your account manager or [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com).
