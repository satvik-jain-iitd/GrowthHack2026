# Reporting and Analytics

Resy Analytics aggregates the data from your business and provides reports on reservations, cover performance, monthly guestbooks, guest surveys and ratings. You can email these reports to specific emails on your distribution list by clicking 'Email Reports' or view them in Resy Analytics. You can also be added to an automated distribution list and receive the report each morning.

### Access 'Analytics' Reports

1) Click "Reports" from the left-hand menu options. 

[[IMAGE: resy-analytics__img01]]  
  

2) You'll see each topic from the "Reports" drop-down list. There are six standard topics: **Home, Reservations, Covers, Ratings, Timing, and Monthly Guestbook.***Please note: To be added to the Analytics email distribution list, select User Permission or click [here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/user-permissions-r1zCWDmU_) for more information.*

### Analytics Standard Topics

  

#### **Home**

The Home page is where you can see today's cover stats at a glance and compare them to yesterday's performance. Scroll through this page to also view recent reviews and past cover trends.

[[IMAGE: resy-analytics__img02]]  

#### **Reservations**

The Reservation report is a list of all upcoming reservations for the selected date. You can choose a date from the date picker at the top of the page. You can sort the list by all guests or VIPs.

[[IMAGE: resy-analytics__img03]]  

  

#### **Covers**

TheCovers page is where you can see a monthly cover breakdown and average covers by service period. 

* Total Covers displays covers for the month broken down by reservations, walk-ins, and waitlist covers. You can also view the number of no show covers and total reservations.
* Average Daily Covers displays your average covers broken down by service period and reservation type. It also displays the difference in covers from the previous month.

[[IMAGE: resy-analytics__img04]]  

  

#### **Ratings**

The Ratings page displays the average guest rating for the restaurant, average guest rating by server, and all individual ratings. After each visit, guests receive an email from Resy asking them to rate their experience on a 5-star scale. Guests have the option to include a comment and choose factors driving their review; Food, Service, Vibe and Other.

* Resy ratings are a closed loop system, meaning that only restaurants are able to see the review and individual star rating. Guests are able to see the all-time star rating online and in the Resy app.

[[IMAGE: resy-analytics__img05]]  

  

#### **Surveys**

Resy offers customizable [Surveys](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/resy-surveys-S1qQMwX8u) to our Platform 360 and Full Stack partners that allow for more in-depth feedback. With Surveys, you can solicit private, 100% customizable feedback with its reservation-integrated, dynamic survey functionality. Guest feedback is tied to a guest name, the wait time, their table number, their server, and their check.

* Resy Surveys builds a direct connection with guests at scale and collects meaningful, useful, and actionable feedback. If you’re interested in learning more about Resy Surveys, reach out to our support team by clicking in the bottom left-hand corner of the screen.

[[IMAGE: resy-analytics__img06]]

  
  

#### **Timing**

The Timing Report shows your average turn times and waitlist stats. 

* Turn times are indicated for all parties sizes every day and service of the week. It's defined as the time from when guest status is changed to "Seated" to when guest status is changed to "Finished". Guest statuses that are never updated to "Finished", or statuses that are changed to Finished after longer than four hours have been excluded from this calculation.

[[IMAGE: resy-analytics__img07]]

  

#### **Monthly Guestbook**

Your Monthly Guestbook includes all guests that have dined in your restaurant in the selected month, sorted by all-time visit count. You can export this list using the Email Report option in the top-right corner. For an all-time guestbook, please email [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com).

#### **Custom Reports**

Platform 360 partners can create custom analytic reports to provide valuable insights into their businesses.

* **Cover Source Reports**: Many restaurants request a custom source report with a more in-depth breakdown of where their guests are coming from (walk-ins, Resy.com, your website, widget via your website, in-house bookings, etc.) This report can help in making decisions where to spend your marketing dollars.
* **Cancellation Report**: Determine if last-minute cancellations are killing your books. A cancellation report can show when you’re seeing the most cancellations and can help in deciding if a cancellation policy on weekends, holidays or large parties should be implemented.

If you’re interested in creating custom reports or upgrading to Platform 360 please reach out to our support team by clicking in the bottom left-hand corner of the screen.
