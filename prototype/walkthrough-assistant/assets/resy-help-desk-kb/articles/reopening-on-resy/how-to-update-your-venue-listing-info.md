# Venue Profile

Edit your restaurant's profile on [Resy.com](https://resy.com/) and in the Resy app. Change the About the Restaurant, Need to Know, Featured Menu Items, and Safety Measures.

**In this article:**

* Update Info through Profile

  

---

  

## Update Info through Profile

#### 1. Log in to the [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/). Select **“Venue”** from the left-hand side menu.

[[IMAGE: how-to-update-your-venue-listing-info__img01]]

2. Click on **"Profile"** to update the About the Restaurant, Need to Know, Tagline, and Images.

[[IMAGE: how-to-update-your-venue-listing-info__img02]]

  

+ Your **About the Restaurant** section is visible to the guest twice when booking a reservation. If on [Resy.com](http://resy.com/) or the Resy app, the guest will see this message before they select a reservation time. This section will also appear on your booking widget. We recommend keeping this section concise and quick to scan
+ **Images** are seen on [Resy.com](http://www.resy.com) and the Resy app and rotate through all the pictures you choose to upload. 10 photos can be added to your Resy listing. The first image will be your main image and included in your confirmation email. It's important that the photos added are .jpeg format with either square or horizontal orientation. The resolution of the image must be a minimum of 800 x 800 pixels and no more than a maximum of 2500 × 2500 pixels
+ The **Need to Know** section is visible on [Resy.com](http://resy.com/) and the Resy app or when the booking widget is opened on your website. Any information you want to convey about safety protocols, parking, walk-ins, etc. should be mentioned here

* **Featured Menu Items**and **Safety Measures** also appear on your profile and during the guest's booking process.**Featured Menu Items**are exclusively listed in a bulleted format on the Details tab when a guest books a reservation.

**Menu Link**  
  
Add a **direct link** to your restaurant's online menu so guests can quickly browse your offerings before booking. Your menu link appears on your venue profile on [Resy.com](http://resy.com/), making it convenient for guests to explore your food and beverage options while deciding where to dine. Keeping this link up to date ensures guests always see your latest menu.  
To add or update your **menu link**:

1. In Resy OS, navigate to Venue → Basic Info.
2. Locate the Menu Link field.
3. Enter the URL for your online menu.
4. Save your changes.

Once saved, your menu link will automatically appear on your venue profile on [Resy.com](http://resy.com/).
