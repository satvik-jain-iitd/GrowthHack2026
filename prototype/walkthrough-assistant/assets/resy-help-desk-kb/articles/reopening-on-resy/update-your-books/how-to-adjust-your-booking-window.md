# Booking Window

Open and close online reservations by changing your booking window. If no reservations are available, adjust your booking window.

**Please note:**Before reopening the booking window, please be sure to check your shift settings in the [dashboard](http://os.resy.com/portal) under **"Service"** to ensure they are up to date prior to releasing online reservations.

**In this article:**

* What is the Booking Window?
* Update Your Booking Window

**What is a Booking Window?**

The Booking Window will determine how far into the future your guests can book reservations. If you are unsure of what to set your booking window to, the standard is 30 days, and you can always adjust from there. 

**Update Your Booking Window**

1. First, you'll log on to the [Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/) using Chrome.
2. Click **"Service"** from the left-hand menu options.
3. Click **"General Settings"** from the drop-down menu options.
4. Scroll to "Booking Window".
5. Update your booking window to your preferred window. This will immediately release your online availability.
6. Click the blue **"Save"** button on the right-hand corner for the booking window to reopen.
