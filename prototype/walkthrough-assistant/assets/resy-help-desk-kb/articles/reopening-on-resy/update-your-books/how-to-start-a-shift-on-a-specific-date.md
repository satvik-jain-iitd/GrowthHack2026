# Start a New Shift on a Specific Date

Create a new shift to start on a specific date in the future from the dashboard. If you're adding brunch or dinner or changing your hours of operations seasonally, create a shift in the future that suits your new style of service.

**Please note**: You must have the “Update Shifts” permission to make these updates. If you don't see “Service” from the left-hand menu in the Dashboard, you'll need to have your [User Permissions](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/user-permissions-r1zCWDmU_) updated.

If you don't have any current shifts or would like to start from scratch for your new shift, you can click [here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-create-and-edit-a-shift-in-dashboard-service-settings-ByNaZwmIu) for steps on how to create a shift from scratch.

## Copying your Shifts

  

[[IMAGE: how-to-start-a-shift-on-a-specific-date__img01]]  

1. Click the three dots on the top-right corner of the shift you wish to copy and select "Copy Shift".
2. In "Step 1 Shift Basics", you'll select the days that coincide with the shift and select the date you would like the shift to start.
3. In "Step 2 Service Settings", you can adjust the floor plans that are on as well as Pacing and Turn Times.
4. In "Step 3 Availability", this is where you can set the Availability (Online, In-house, and Walk-in) of the tables on the floor plan. Click [here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-manage-shift-availability-rJk3ZDQ8_) for more information.
5. In "Step 4 Reservation Settings", here is where you create policies for the entire shift.
6. In "Step 5 Custom Policies", you create Custom Policies for specific groups of tables.
7. Click "Update Shift", and your new shift will be listed in Shift Setting under Upcoming Shifts.

**Please note**: If you don't want to continue using your active shifts until the start date of the new shift, you'll want to delete your current shift after you've created the new shift.
