# Customize Turn Times

Your Turn Times determine the maximum amount of time that is allotted for each party size in your shift to dine. Reservation times with pacing at capacity will not be bookable online. When booking in-house, pacing will turn red when its reached capacity as a visual indicator to choose or offer a more optimal time. Internally, you can make reservations that exceed pacing.

**Edit Pacing for a Recurring Base Shift**

1. First, you'll log on to the [Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/login) using Chrome.
2. Click **"Service"** from the left-hand menu options.
3. Click **“Shift Settings”** from the drop-down menu options.
4. Click the three dots icon next to the shift you'd like to edit and choose “Edit”.
5. Under “Step 2 Service Settings” click **“Turn Times”**.
6. Once you make the edits to your Turn Times, click **“Update Shift”**.

**Customize Turn Times by Table**

1. First, you'll log on to the [Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/login) using Chrome.
2. Click **"Service"** from the left-hand menu options.
3. Click **“Shift Settings”** from the drop-down menu options.
4. Click the three dots icon next to the shift you'd like to edit and choose **“Edit”**.
5. Go to “Step 5 Custom Policies.”
6. Click **“Customize Tables”**. If you have an existing Custom Policy that you want to edit the Turn Times in, click the pencil icon next to the policy*.*
7. Select the tables you would like to give custom Turn Times.
8. Click “Next, Customize Policies”.
9. Enter the reservation name for these tables.
10. Once you make the edits to your Turn Times, click **“Save”**.
11. Click **“Update Shift”** to save these updates.

**Edit Turn Times for a Single Day or Shift**

1. First, you'll log on to the [Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/login) using Chrome.
2. Click **"Service"** from the left-hand menu options.
3. Click **“Calendar”** from the drop-down menu options.
4. Click on the date you'd like to edit and select **“Single Day Edit”**.
5. Select the shift.
6. Follow the steps in the section above to edit your Turn Times.

When you’ve completed a single day edit you'll see the edit reflected on the calendar date.
