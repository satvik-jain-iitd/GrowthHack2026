# Setting up Slots

The Resy Slots feature is available for restaurant partners on our 360 monthly service plans. If you'd like to consider an upgrade, please contact your Resy Account Manager or chat Resy Support. 

**You can****use Slots in Resy to:**

* Build “Manager Slots” to more clearly designate manager holds on your shifts.
* Offer a unique experience at a specific time on your tables keeping the rest of the table as standard service throughout the shift.
* Update your shifts to exclusively use slots to manage your reservations.

**Setting up Slots in the Resy Dashboard**

You can build and manage your slots from the [Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/login).

1. From the left-hand menu, click **“Service"**.
2. Then for recurring shifts click **“Shift Settings”** or for single-day click **“Calendar"**.
3. From the shift editor, navigate to “Step 3 Availability".
4. At the bottom of the availability page click **“More Customization”** to open the detailed availability view.

[[IMAGE: how-to-setup-slots-in-the-resy-dashboard__img01]]  
5. You'll see the “Base Availability” tab where you can set the underlying availability for your tables. You can access the slots editor by clicking the “Slots” tab on the top right.

[[IMAGE: how-to-setup-slots-in-the-resy-dashboard__img02]]  
6. From the Slots editor, hover over the time you’d like to create a Slot on the desired table. You'll see a “+” icon appear and you can click the icon to add the Slot.

[[IMAGE: how-to-setup-slots-in-the-resy-dashboard__img03]]  
7. When you've added a new Slot, a side panel to the left will open up allowing you to choose the details for your Slot addition. Select the min/max party size, online or in-house and toggle on the “'Manager Slot” attribute (only in-house slots can be designated as manager slots).

[[IMAGE: how-to-setup-slots-in-the-resy-dashboard__img04]]  
8. Click **“Update Availability”** in the bottom right corner and then **“Update Shift”** to save.

**Customize by Slot in the Resy Dashboard**

Through the [Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/login), you have the ability to customize by slot. This gives you the option to edit specific details to one or more of the slots on your shift.

1. In the Dashboard click **“Service"**.
2. Then for recurring shifts, click **“Shift Settings”**or for single day, click **“Calendar"**.
3. From the shift editor, navigate to “Step 5 Custom Policies".
4. Select **“Customize Slots"**.

  
[[IMAGE: how-to-setup-slots-in-the-resy-dashboard__img06]]  
5. Check off one or more slots to customize and click “Next” in the bottom right corner.

[[IMAGE: how-to-setup-slots-in-the-resy-dashboard__img07]]  
6. From here you can edit every attribute of the selected slots, such as:

* Editing the “Reservation Name” to label the experience to your guests online
* Adjusting the turn time specific to the selected slots
* Adding payment for a prepaid experience
* Applying a cancellation fee policy
* Customizing the language your guests will see when making a reservation on the selected slot

[[IMAGE: how-to-setup-slots-in-the-resy-dashboard__img08]]  

7. Click **“Save”** in the bottom-right corner and then **“Update Shift”** to complete.
