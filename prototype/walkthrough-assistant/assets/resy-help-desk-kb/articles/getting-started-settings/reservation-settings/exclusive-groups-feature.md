# Exclusive Groups Feature

Exclusive Groups allows you to allocate online reservations to a select group of guests. One or more of your VIP, Concierge, and Guest tags can be used to create an online group where only guests with those tags will be able to see those reservations. These groups can be applied to your tables in the availability section of your shift settings.

  

**Please note:** Restaurants with our Platform 360 package have access to this feature. Please email [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com) if you'd like to upgrade your package.

**How Restaurants are Using Exclusive Groups**

* **VIPs** - Guests with a VIP tag are your most valuable diners. Creating a VIP group will give your most important guests exclusive options to book online
* **Regulars**- Use a Regular VIP tag to create a group to offer extra online booking options to your repeat diners
* **Concierges**- Group one or more of your concierge tags to offer more online options to partnering concierge groups
* **Guest Tags** - Use your Guest Tags to create exclusive groups for booking specific guests with those unique tags

## **Setting Up Booking Groups**

1. In your ResyOS Dashboard, click **"Guests"** from the left-hand menu options.
2. Click **"Exclusive Groups"**.
3. Click **“Create a Group”** to get started.  
  
[[IMAGE: exclusive-groups-feature__img01]]
4. Name the group. This is an internal name that will be listed in your availability settings when applying the group to your shifts.
5. Click on one or more of your VIP, Concierge, or Guest Tags to assign them to the group.Groups can be specific to one tag or allow access for multiple tags.
6. Choose the expiration time for your group.   
  
[[IMAGE: exclusive-groups-feature__img02]]
7. Click **“Create Group”** in the bottom-right corner.

**Please note:**When the expiration time elapses, the table will return to the base availability you have set up for the shift. 

## **View and Manage Groups**

The main view on the Exclusive Group section of the Resy Dashboard will show you all of your existing channels. You can edit or delete a group by clicking the 3 horizontal dots on the right.

[[IMAGE: exclusive-groups-feature__img03]]

## Add Exclusive Groups to Your Shifts

Use the Service Settings in the Resy Dashboard to assign your channels to a recurring shift or as a single day shift. 

**Applying groups to your recurring shifts**:

1. First, you'll log on to the [Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/login) using Chrome.
2. Click**“Service”** from the left-hand menu options.
3. Click **"Shift Settings"** from the drop-down menu options.
4. Click the three horizontal dots on the shift and select **“Edit Shift”**.

**Applying groups to a single day:**

1. Click**“Service”** from the left-hand menu options.
2. Select **“Calendar”** from the drop-down.
3. Click on the date you'd like to edit.
4. Select **“Single Day Edit”**.
5. From “Step 3 Availability” in the shift editor, click **“More Customization”**. [[IMAGE: exclusive-groups-feature__img04]]
6. Select the tab labeled**“Exclusive Groups”** on the top right. This section will display the table attributes you have set up as the base availability for reference (i.e., Online, In-House, or Walk-In).[[IMAGE: exclusive-groups-feature__img05]]
7. Click a table number or select specific times on a table throughout the shift and choose from the groups you have set up on the left side panel. From the side panel, you can also edit the base availability to online or in-house if you choose.

**Please note**: When a group expires, the tables will always default to the base availability applied to that shift.  
[[IMAGE: exclusive-groups-feature__img06]]

8. Click **“Update Availability”** in the bottom-right corner and **“Update Shift”** to save. 

**Please note**: The tables you apply an Exclusive Group to will have the channel’s icon on the timeline view. The same icon will display in the timeline view on the Resy OS iPad App to indicate the table has a group applied.
