# Automated Reservation Emails

Guests booking or making reservation changes online will receive an automated email from Resy with the reservation details every time they book, change, or cancel a reservation. We've listed examples below of the emails guests receive along with the options to customize the content.

**Please note:**The email customization feature is only available for restaurants on our Platform 360 monthly service plan. If you're interested in upgrading your account, please contact us at [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com) or contact your Resy Account Manager.

## **Booked Email**

Below is an example of the standard Resy “Booked” email. 

To customize the body of the email: 

1. Access the [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues) using Chrome.
2. Click "Service" from the left-hand menu.
3. Click "Communications".
4. Choose Confirmation, Cancelation, Change, or Waitlist.

## **Edit Email**

Below is an example of the standard Resy “Change” email. 

To customize the body of the email: 

1. Access the [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues) using Chrome.
2. Click "Service" from the left-hand menu and click "Communications".
3. Locate the "Change" email.

## **Cancel Email**

Below is an example of the standard Resy “Cancel” email. 

To customize the body of the email: 

1. Access the [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues) using Chrome.
2. Click "Service" from the left-hand menu and click "Communictions".
3. Locate the "Cancel" email.

[[IMAGE: standard-resy-automated-email-content__img03]]

**Please note**: Cancellation policy language in the footer of Resy emails cannot be customized. The cancellation language will auto-populate in the footer of the email based on the policy you've set for the table in your shift settings.
