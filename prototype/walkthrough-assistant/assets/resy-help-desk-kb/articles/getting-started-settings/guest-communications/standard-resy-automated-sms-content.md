# Text Message Automation

All Resy automatic confirmation text language and timing are standardized. 

* The language and timing for all automated reminder SMS, except the Follow-up Reminder SMS, can be customized for Platform 360 partners through the online [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues) under Guest Communications. [Learn about Customized Text Messages](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/customizing-text-message-content-HJP6WwQLO). Contact your Account Manager or Resy Support ([resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com)) if you’d like to upgrade your monthly service package.

## **Text Messages Content**

### **Confirmation Text**

Resy guests will automatically receive the following confirmation text from Resy that asks guests to **confirm or cancel** their reservation through SMS, saving you time from manual confirmations and encouraging timely cancellations for rebooking time slots.

"Just a reminder you're booked at (Restaurant Name) for (Service Period) tomorrow. Your table is for (Party Size) at (Time).Please reply "1" to confirm your booking or "9" to cancel. You can make other changes in the Resy App."

1. **If the guest replies "1" to confirm**: They’ll receive a text saying,"Great, done. Thanks."
2. **If the guest replies "9" to cancel**: They’ll receive a text saying,"Your reservation at (Restaurant Name) has been cancelled. Thanks for letting us know."

 [[IMAGE: standard-resy-automated-sms-content__img01]]

### **Day of Reservation Reminder Text**

"You're due at (Restaurant Name) in (30/60) minutes."

Timing: 

* **60 minutes prior to the reservation time:**Restaurants in PST & Miami receive the SMS reminder 60 minutes prior to the reservation time.
* **30 minutes prior to the reservation time:**Restaurants not in PST receive the SMS reminder 30 minutes prior to the reservation time.

 

### **Waitlist Text**

Resy waitlist guests will automatically receive a text from Resy anytime they are added to a venue's waitlist.

*"You've been added to the waitlist at (Restaurant Name)! The estimated wait for your party of (Party Size) is (Quoted Time). We'll text you when your table is ready. Text 9 at any time to remove yourself from the waitlist."*

*[[IMAGE: standard-resy-automated-sms-content__img02]]*

Guests will also get a text message once their waitlist status changes to **Wait: Table Ready**. 

*"Your table at (Restaurant Name) is ready! We'll hold it for 10 minutes. Please text "1" to confirm or "9" if you are no longer able to make it, and we'll remove you from the waitlist."*

## **Confirmation Text Timing**

### **Dinner Reservations**

* **4pm, the day prior to the reservation:**Guests who book a dinner reservation by 3pm the day prior to the reservation date will receive the confirmation SMS at this time.
	+ **12pm, follow-up confirmation:** If guests **do not reply** to the previous reminder SMS text, they will receive a follow-up text at 12pm the day of the reservation. This timing and text content cannot currently be edited under Guest Communications.
* **12pm, the day of the reservation:** Guests who book a dinner reservation the day prior to the reservation date after 3pm will get the confirmation SMS at this time.
* **Day of reservation:**Guests who book a dinner reservation on the day of are considered confirmed and **do not**receive a confirmation SMS.

### **Brunch/Lunch Reservations**

* **10am, day of the reservation:**Guests who booked prior to the date of the reservation will receive the confirmation SMS at this time.
* **Day of Reservation:**Guests who book a Lunch/Brunch reservation the day of are considered confirmed and **do not**receive a confirmation SMS.

### **Breakfast Reservations**

* **9pm, the day prior to the reservation:**Guests who book a breakfast reservation by 8pm the day prior to the reservation date will receive the confirmation SMS at this time.
* **Reservations made after 8pm:**Guests who book a breakfast reservation after 8pm the day prior are considered confirmed and **do not**receive a confirmation SMS.
