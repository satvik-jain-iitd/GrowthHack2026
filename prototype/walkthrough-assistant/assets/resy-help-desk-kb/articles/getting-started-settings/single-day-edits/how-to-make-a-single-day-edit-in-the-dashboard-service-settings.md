# Single Day Shift Updates in the Resy OS Portal

Update a single shift to change your hours or edit your plan for a holiday or special service. Learn how to close days in the dashboard [here.](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-close-days-in-dashboard-ry4VzPQ8O)

**Please note:** For access to the Dashboard Service Settings, the login you use must have the correct user permissions. To request a change to your current login, please reach out to a member of your own team who has the "Manage Users" permission.

  

**In this article:**

* [How to Update Single Day Shifts](#part1)
* [Copy Single Day Shift Updates](#part2)

  

## **How to Update Single Day Shifts in the Resy Dashboard**

1. Click “Service” from the left-hand menu to open your Service Settings.
2. Click “Calendar” from the drop-down, then click the day you'd like to edit and select "Single Day Edit". From there, you can edit every aspect of the current shift for this day.
3. Click “Save” in the bottom-right corner to finish.

**Please note:**

* Single Day Edits will **only**change the shift for the specific day chosen.
* Edits to your default shift can be done from the "Shift Settings" tab.
* The edits you make to a shift will be live as soon as you hit "Save" if the shift is within your online booking window. The ResyOS App may take a few minutes to reflect the changes. However, you can force close and re-open the app to see the changes right away.
* Any reservations that have already been booked on a shift that you are editing will remain intact. If you changed any reservation policy details (adding a cancellation fee for example), guests that were already reserved will have the original reservation details that they booked with listed in the reservation profile.

  

## **Copy Single Day Shift Updates in the Dashboard**

Use this feature to replicate the same hours or floor plan on another day.

1. From the left-hand menu, select “Service”, then “Calendar”.
2. Scroll to and select the Single Day Edit you would like to copy.

[[IMAGE: how-to-make-a-single-day-edit-in-the-dashboard-service-settings__img01]]

3. Hover your cursor over the edited shift and select the copy icon. 

 [[IMAGE: how-to-make-a-single-day-edit-in-the-dashboard-service-settings__img02]]

4. Enter the date you'd like to copy the Single Day Edit to and then click “Copy Shift” in the bottom-right corner to save.

[[IMAGE: how-to-make-a-single-day-edit-in-the-dashboard-service-settings__img03]]

Your new Single Day Edit is complete! You'll now see on the Calendar view that your Single Day Edit has been applied to the new date.
