# Single Day Floor Plan Updates

Change your floor plan and edit tables for a special shift or single day by copying your floorplan first and making changes to the new one. 

---

## **Duplicate Your Floor Plan**

**Please note:**This will be done in the Resy OS app on your iPad. 

Start by accessing the “Edit Floor Plan” tool from the iPad dropdown menu.

1. Tap the venue name in the top-left corner.
2. Select “Edit Floor Plan”.
3. Tap the floor plan name in the top middle of screen then “Manage Floor Plans”.
4. Tap the Floor Plan you wish to use.
5. Tap the two page icon to duplicate the Floor Plan.
6. Enter the name of the new Floor Plan.
7. Click “X” to exit out of Manage Floor Plan.
8. Tap the Floor Plan name in the top center of the screen and select the newly named Floor Plan.
9. Make your Floor Plan edits. Click [here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-create-and-edit-floor-plans-in-the-resyos-app-Hk16bwXUO) for more information on how to edit a Floorplan.
10. Click “Save”.

After creating the duplicate Floor Plan, you'll want to activate it in the shift. Here are some [steps](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-activate-floor-plan-and-edit-table-availability-for-a-single-day-H1Q3Wv7IO) on how to activate your Floor Plan for a Single Day.
