# Difference Between Automatic and Manual Assignment

Reservations booked online are automatically assigned to tables, change the table assignment on your iPad.

**Please note**: The “Show Resy assigned tables on reservation list and floor plan” must be toggled on in Reservation Settings for this option to show.

**Resy Auto Assigned Tables vs User Assigned Tables**

* **Resy Auto Assign Tables**: Reservations that are automatically assigned to a table based on inventory when the reservation is made. These appear in *italics*.
* **User Assigned Tables:** Reservations that are assigned to tables in-house by a Resy OS user. These appears in bolded white.

  
  
[[IMAGE: how-to-know-if-a-reservation-was-automatically-or-manually-assigned__img01]]  
**Viewing Resy Auto Assigned Tables**

You can choose to see which table assignments were made in-house by a Resy OS user or auto assigned by toggling on “Show Resy assigned tables on reservation list and floor plan”.

1. Tap the restaurant name in the top left corner of Resy OS to reveal the Venue Settings drop-down.
2. Select “Settings,” then “Reservations."
3. Toggle on “Show Resy assigned tables on reservation list and floor plan.”

  
[[IMAGE: how-to-know-if-a-reservation-was-automatically-or-manually-assigned__img02]]  

You can hide Resy auto assigned tables from showing on tables in your floor plan:

1. Select “Floor Plan” view.
2. Tap on the vertical three dots towards the upper right-hand corner to display customizations.
3. Tap “Hide Resy auto-assigned reservations” **or** “Show Resy auto-assigned reservations” for reservations to re-appear.

**Please note**: The “Show Resy assigned tables on reservation list and floor plan” must be toggled on in Settings for this option to show as available in the display customizations menu.
