# How do I show my Table Combinations in the Timeline View in the Resy OS App?

Tap "Show Table Combinations" from the menu dropdown in the top-right corner to display your combined tables on the timeline.

[[IMAGE: how-to-show-table-combinations-on-the-timeline-view-in-resyos__img01]]

  
With this setting enabled, you can drag and drop to assign reservations and book directly into your table combinations from the timeline view. When one or more of the tables that make up the combination is reserved, the combination will show as “Reserved” on the timeline indicating the full combination is not free to book.

[[IMAGE: how-to-show-table-combinations-on-the-timeline-view-in-resyos__img02]]
