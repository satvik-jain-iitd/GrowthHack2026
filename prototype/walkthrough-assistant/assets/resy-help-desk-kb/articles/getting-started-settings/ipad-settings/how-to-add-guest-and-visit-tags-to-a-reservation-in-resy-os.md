# Create and Add Guest and Visit Tags to a Reservation

**Visit Tags**and **Guest Tags** are an easy way to keep guest profiles and reservations up to date. Tags are shorthand and visual cues to the door team about the guest. Birthday, Nut Allergy, Regular, Friend of Chef are examples of common tags.  

* **Visit Tags:** Live on a particular reservation only. For example: Birthday, anniversary, graduation, etc.
* **Guest Tags:** Live on a guest profile indefinitely. For example: Regular, VIP, friend of house, Nut Allergy, etc.

  

---

## 

## **How to Create a New Guest/Visit Tag**

1. In the Resy OS App, tap your restaurant name in the top-left corner.
2. Tap “Settings”.
3. Tap “Tags”.
4. Tap “+ Create ___ Tag”.
5. Enter the tag name.
6. Select the tag type.
7. Tap “Create Tag”.

**Please note**: Toggle on “Notable Tag” if you want the tag you’re creating to be highlighted on the reservation list when applied to visits and to be printed on your chit.

**More About Guest and Visit Tags**

* Allergy, Dietary Restrictions, Dietary Preferences, and Identification tags will be shared with your Venue Group.
* Relationship and General tags will be venue specific.

This share icon indicates which tags are shared:

  
  
[[IMAGE: how-to-add-guest-and-visit-tags-to-a-reservation-in-resy-os__img02]]

**Please note**: You can tap on any tag that your venue has created then tap “Delete Tag” to delete it. You can also tap on a tag your venue has created to edit the tag. You are not able to delete or edit a tag created by Resy.

## 

## **Ways to Add Tags to Guest and Reservation Profiles**

* Both Guest and Visit tags can be added when booking the reservation in the Booking Module while viewing the guest profile.
* Both Guest and Visit tags can also be added by tapping the reservation on the timeline or floorplan view.
* Guest tags can also be added directly by searching a guest on the guestbook, tapping their profile, and adding from there.

**Adding a Guest Tag from the Booking Module**

1. Tap the plus sign to the right of “Guest”.
2. Tap “Guest Tag”.
3. Tap the tags you want to add.
4. Once the tag has been added, tap the back arrow on the pop-up message to close and continue booking the reservation.

 **[[IMAGE: how-to-add-guest-and-visit-tags-to-a-reservation-in-resy-os__img03]]**

  

#### **Adding a Guest Tag from the Guest Book Profile**

1. Tap your restaurant name in the top-left corner.
2. Tap “Guest Book”.
3. Enter the guest’s name, phone number, or email address, or tap “Create New Guest” if the guest doesn’t have a profile yet.
4. Tap the plus sign to the right of “Guest”.
5. Tap “Guest Tag”.
6. Tap the tags you want to add.
7. Once the tag has been added, tap the back arrow on the pop-up message to close and continue booking the reservation.

  

**[[IMAGE: how-to-add-guest-and-visit-tags-to-a-reservation-in-resy-os__img04]]**

**Adding a Visit Tag from the Booking Module**

1. Tap the plus sign to the right of “Visit”.
2. Tap “Visit Tags”.
3. Tap the tags you want to add.
4. Once the tag has been added, tap the back arrow on the pop-up message to close and continue booking the reservation.

  

**[[IMAGE: how-to-add-guest-and-visit-tags-to-a-reservation-in-resy-os__img05]]**

  

**Guest and Visit Tags can be added from the Reservation Profile**

1. Tap the guests name on the Booked List.
2. Tap the plus sign (+) to the right of “Guest” or “Visit”.
3. Tap Guest or Visit Tag.
4. Tap the tags you want to add.
5. Once the tag has been added, tap the back arrow on the pop-up message to close and continue booking the reservation.'

  

**[[IMAGE: how-to-add-guest-and-visit-tags-to-a-reservation-in-resy-os__img06]]**
