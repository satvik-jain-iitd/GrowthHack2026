# Password Reset for Resy OS

## There are two ways that you can reset your password.

  
**Option 1:**

Go to the [Dashboard login page](https://os.resy.com/portal/) and click on “Forgot Password?”

[[IMAGE: how-to-reset-your-password__img01]]  

  

**Option 2**: 

If you’re already logged in, click on your initials in the upper right-hand corner and click on "Reset Password".

[[IMAGE: how-to-reset-your-password__img02]]
