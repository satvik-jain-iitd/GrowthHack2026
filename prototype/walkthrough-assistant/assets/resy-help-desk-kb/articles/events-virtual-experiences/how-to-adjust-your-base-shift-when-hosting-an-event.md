# How do I adjust my normal shift when I'm hosting an event that changes availability?

Whether you're hosting a General Admission or Assigned Seating event, if you choose to run your event alongside normal service be sure to adjust your base shift to reflect the correct availability with a Single Day Edit.

[[IMAGE: how-to-adjust-your-base-shift-when-hosting-an-event__img01]]

  
If your event is being held on a separate floor plan like a private dining room or your patio that is not usually bookable, you're all set!

[[IMAGE: how-to-adjust-your-base-shift-when-hosting-an-event__img02]]  

If your event is taking place on your standard floor plan, you'll want to make sure those tables are not bookable as regular dining tables. 

1. Click “Service”from the left-hand menu, then "Calendar" in the dashboard and create a [Single Day Edit](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-make-a-single-day-edit-in-the-dashboard-service-settings-rJf6bD7IO).[[IMAGE: how-to-adjust-your-base-shift-when-hosting-an-event__img03]]
2. Scroll to the date of your event and click into it. From the menu, select “Single Day Edit”. [[IMAGE: how-to-adjust-your-base-shift-when-hosting-an-event__img04]]
3. Click into “Step 3 Availability” and set the tables you're using for your event to “Walk-In only” and “Update Shift”. Then you're all set!

[[IMAGE: how-to-adjust-your-base-shift-when-hosting-an-event__img05]]
