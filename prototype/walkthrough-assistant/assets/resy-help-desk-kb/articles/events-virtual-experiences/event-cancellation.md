# Event Cancellation

#### **How do I cancel an event?**

Once in the online dashboard, take the following steps:

1. Click "**Events**" from the left-hand menu.
2. Click "**Live****Events**" from the drop-down menu.
3. Click the three dots next to your live event.
4. Click "**Cancel Event**".

[[IMAGE: event-cancellation__img01]]

***Please note****: If the event has bookings, when you cancel the booking, guests will receive a cancellation email and receive a refund (if the event was prepaid).*

---

#### 

#### What happens if I cancel an event?

All reservations will be cancelled and automatically refunded if the event is prepaid.

---

#### 

#### Can I view a list of reservations once the event has been cancelled?

No, you'll need to access the Reservation list before cancelling.

1. Log on to the [Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/login) using Chrome.
2. Choose “**Reports**” from the left-hand menu options.
3. Choose “**Reservations**” > Choose Date.
4. "**Email Report**".

---

#### 

#### **Can I copy a cancelled event?**

Yes, to copy a cancelled event follow the steps below:

1. From the “**Events**” option, click "**Past Events**".
2. Click the three dots next to your cancelled event.
3. Click "**Copy Event**".

[[IMAGE: event-cancellation__img02]]

***Please note****: There is a maximum of three events per shift. This includes live and cancelled events. If you've reached this maximum and need to delete a cancelled event, please reach out to [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com).*
