# Assigned Seating Event

Create an Assigned Seating Event for wine dinners, chef collabs and more. Best for seated events where you want to prescribe seating times and limit the number of guests by time.

**In this article:**

* [Step 1: Event Basics](#part1)
* [Step 2: Service Settings](#part2)
* [Step 3: Ticket Settings](#part3)
* [Step 4: Availability](#part4)
* [Step 5: Sales Period](#part5)
* [Step 6: Event Marketing](#part6)

---

First, log in to the Resy Dashboard using the Chrome Browser. 

1. Click **“Events”** from the left-hand menu options.
2. Click any of the drop-down menu options.
3. Click **“Create Event”** or **“New Event”.**
4. Select **“Assigned Seating”** Event to begin creating your Event.

---

  

### Step 1. Event Basics

* **Event Name:**Name your event using up to 85 characters. The name you choose will be guest facing on [Resy.com](http://resy.com/), the Resy booking widget and the Resy app.
* **Event Date:** Enter the date of your event.
* **Event First Seating/Last Seating Time:** The first seating and last seating time of your event can be set more broadly than the specific reservation times. For example, if you're creating a single seating event at 6:00 pm you may want the start time to be 5:00 pm so that you can seat guests early. We’ll choose the specific reservation times using your pacing and availability in Steps 2 and 3.
* **Choose to Replace or Run Alongside Your Standard Service:**If you have a standard recurring shift or shifts that overlap with your event, you’ll be given these options:
	1. **This Event Will Replace Normal Service:** Choosing this option will automatically take your overlapping base shift offline for the day. Any pre-existing reservations you may have will still be accessible on the shift in Resy OS, but will be listed as unassigned tables.
	2. **This Event Will Run Alongside Normal Service in a Portion of my Restaurant:** Choosing this option will leave your standard shift as is. It is up to you, after creating your event, to make a single day edit to your base shift on this date to adjust your availability as needed. *For a detailed walkthrough on best practices, visit*[*How do I adjust my normal shift when I’m hosting an event that changes availability?*](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-adjust-your-base-shift-when-hosting-an-event-rySYbv7Ld)[[IMAGE: how-to-create-an-assigned-seating-event__img01]]
* **Milestone Dates:**  Set your event milestone dates**.**Choose the date and time the event can be viewed, and the date and time tickets will go on sale.[[IMAGE: how-to-create-an-assigned-seating-event__img02]]
* **Is this event private?**  Hide an event on Resy.com and make it accessible via the link that is shared by the venue. **Please note:**The Private Event page can be accessed by anyone who has the link. For further privacy, you may consider Exclusive Groups if you're on the Platform 360 package.

---

  

### Step 2. Service Settings

* **Floor Plan:** Choose one or more of your floor plans for your assigned seating event. If you need to create a new floor plan for your event, you'll need to create the floor plan using the floor plan editor in the ResyOS app on your iPad or in the Dashboard. Once a floor plan is created, it will appear on this list to select. Here’s more information on [How to Create and Edit Floor Plans in the ResyOS App and on Web.](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-create-and-edit-floor-plans-in-the-resyos-app-Hk16bwXUO)
* **Pacing:**Click “Customize Intervals” to set pacing for each 15-minute interval. For events with specific seating times, use your pacing to drive covers only to the seating times. For flexible seating, set your pacing as you would a standard shift.
* *For more information, learn how to set up fixed seating times for your event*[here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-setup-fixed-seating-times-for-your-shifts-H1v2ZPm8O)*and**[How do I use Pacing and Availability to boost ticket sales for my Event?](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-optimize-seating-times-for-maximum-event-ticket-sales-ry1cP7Id)*

### Step 3. Ticket Settings

Create one or more ticket types for your event. Only one ticket type is required and each ticket type you create will represent a different experience you'd like to offer for this event. Each ticket type can have a unique price, ticket name, content highlights, turn times, and cancellation/refund policy.

**Create Your Ticket Types:**

1. Click “Create Ticket” to get started.
2. Enter the details of the ticket package and click “Create Ticket” to save. You'll see a summary of the ticket packages created along with the package details.
3. When you've completed creating one or more Ticket Packages, you'll be prompted to apply them to the desired tables on your floor plan for the event.[[IMAGE: how-to-create-an-assigned-seating-event__img03]]
4. Minimum/Maximum Ticket Controls: This is how you’ll control the minimum and maximum amount of tickets a guest  can book for an event.

**Please note:** If you don’t edit this setting, the Minimum/Maximum ticket controls default to the minimum/maximum set in your Listing Info. 

[[IMAGE: how-to-create-an-assigned-seating-event__img04]]

**Set Your Off-Market Time:**This is the time in which any tables that have not yet been booked will automatically come offline ahead of your event. After the “off-market time” cancellations will not put new availability back online.

**[[IMAGE: how-to-create-an-assigned-seating-event__img05]]**

**Delete Ticket Type on Live Event:** You’ll now be able to remove a ticket type from an event that has already gone live. 

**Please** **note:** a ticket type can only be deleted if no tickets of that type have been purchased.

---

  

### Step 4. Availability

By default your tables for the event will be set to online and you can adjust by table type from the main view. Click “More Customization” to set specific availability by individual table and time.

* For fixed seating times, you'll want to edit your availability so that only your desired seating times are bookable online with the remainder set to “walk-in”
* For flexible availability, you'll want to be sure to indicate any tables or times held back as “in-house” and any portion of the restaurant you'd like reserved for walk-ins as “walk-in”
* *For more information, learn how to set up fixed seating times for your event*[here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-setup-fixed-seating-times-for-your-shifts-H1v2ZPm8O)

### Step 5. Sales Period

* Pre-sale

Do you want to enable pre-sale for an exclusive booking group? On this date, your event page will be viewable to the public but only guests in the selected channel can book tickets. 

**Please note:** The Sales Period step, as well as Exclusive Groups, are only available on the Platform 360 plan.

### Step 6. Event Marketing

**Photos:**Choose images to feature on your event listing page on [Resy.com](http://resy.com/) and in the Resy app.

* Import up to six images or choose from the Resy Themes.
* We support JPG and JPEG image files only.
* **Content:**Describe the details of your event to your guests.
* **Event Summary (Required):** Sell your event to guests with a punchy description that invites guests to learn more.
* **About This Event (Required):** Give your guests all the details! A detailed and accurate “About” section increases the likelihood of ticket sales and guest satisfaction.
* **Need to Know (Optional):** Let guests know about important transportation, cancellation, or other policies related to your event.
* **Double Confirmation (Optional): Y**our guests will receive a final pop-up message upon attempting to confirm the booking.This message can be used to highlight strict cancellation policies, 21+ policies, special seating requirements, or other important information.[[IMAGE: how-to-create-an-assigned-seating-event__img06]]

  

**Click “Schedule Event” in the bottom right corner to finish creating your event.**

Upon saving your event, you'll see a pop-up window that will reference the live date and list other actions you may want to take to finalize your setup.

**Set Event Content:** Click here to edit or add event content.

**Customize Guest Communications:** Here you can set a custom SMS reminder and email communications for guests that book your event. If you do not create custom communications for the event, the standard SMS and emails will be used. *For a detailed walkthrough visit*[*How do I customize Guest Communications for Events?*](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-customize-guest-communications-for-events-r1vtWwm8_)

**Adjust Other Shifts on This Day:** Clicking here will guide you to your base shifts for that date to make any necessary adjustments to your availability. *For a detailed walkthrough on best practices, visit*[*How do I adjust my normal shift when I'm hosting an event that changes availability?*](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-adjust-your-base-shift-when-hosting-an-event-rySYbv7Ld)
