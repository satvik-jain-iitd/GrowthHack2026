# Manage and Edit Add-on Items

Once your Add-on Item has been created, you can manage and edit these Add-on Items in the "Item Library".

You can access your Items Library in the [Online Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues):

1. Click "Venue" from the left-hand menu.
2. Click “Prepaid Items”.

**Please note:** When you make an edit to an existing add-on item, this will affect all shifts that the item is being used and all transactions going forward.

## **What is the Item Type?**

The **Item Type** is related to the **Tax Code** that matches your Item.

* Alcoholic Beverage
* Book
* Candy (does not contain flour)
* Chocolate (does not contain flour)
* Clothing
* Donation
* Food
* Grocery/Pantry
* Gum
* Merchandise
* Non-Alcoholic Beverage
* On-Premise Event
* Online Event

Single-Use Items (e.g., napkins, utensils)

## **Managing your Item Library**

### Ways to use your Item Library:

* Creating, updating, or deleting items
* Checking the quantities left on your items
* Visibility on where your active items are currently being offered

  

### How to manage your Item Library

  
[[IMAGE: how-can-i-manage-add-on-items__img01]]

* Item: Shows the name of each item and their cost
* Applied To: Shows which shifts you have applied these items. (Click “Details” to view the shifts where the Items are active.)
* Total Stock: Shows the total amount of that item you have in stock
* Daily Limit: If you have a daily max of that item you would like sold, it would show here
* Date Created: Shows the date you added the item to the Library

You can add an item in the Item Library by clicking “Add Item”

**You can edit an existing Item by tapping the pencil icon next to the item.**

**Please note**: When you make an edit to an existing add-on item, this will affect all shifts that the item is being used and all transactions going forward

  

**You can copy an existing Item by tapping the 3 dots.**

[[IMAGE: how-can-i-manage-add-on-items__img02]]

**You can delete an existing Item by tapping the three dots next to the item.**

### **Example**:

* Total Stock Quantity = 50.
* Daily Sales Limit = 10.
* Sales will continue daily, max 10 items daily, until 50 items have been sold overall.
