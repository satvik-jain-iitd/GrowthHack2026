# Resy x Google Integration

**How to Have Your Resy Availability Appear on Google**

The Google Knowledge Panel buttons and links are automated and adjust on their own based on traffic and click throughs. As reservations are booked through Resy, it will typically update within a few days to a couple weeks from when you are live on Resy. You'll also want to make sure you have no other online inventory available on another booking system, if that applies. 

**Your availability will not show on Google if:**

* Your Resy shifts are outside of the Google Business Hours listed.
* Your Google Listing isn't set to accept dine-in.

[[IMAGE: google-integration__img01]]

**NOTE:** For new openings, you must claim your Google Business Profile first. Step by step instructions are provided by Google [here](https://support.google.com/business/answer/14189260). Changes on Google can take 24-72 hours to update.

### **Important information about Paid Reservations on Reserve with Google:**

* Bookings for reservations without a cancellation or prepaid policy will be completed in Google and managed (modified/cancelled) in Google. The diner will receive confirmation emails from Resy and Google for these bookings.
* Reservations with cancellation fees will show on Google and users will be redirected to [resy.com](http://www.resy.com) to complete the booking.
* Prepaid inventory will show on Google. To complete the booking, diners will be redirected to [resy.com](http://www.resy.com) to input reservation and payment details. All reservation confirmation emails will come from Resy, not Google, for these bookings.
* Reservations booked with payment (Cancellation Fees and Prepaid inventory) will be completed in Resy and must be managed in Resy. They cannot be modified or cancelled via Google.
* This new redirect will ensure policies and reservation information will be shown to the diner.

### **Booking Flow for Inventory with Payment:**

[[IMAGE: google-integration__img02]]

**Please note:**For all paid inventory that is being redirected to [resy.com](http://resy.com/) to book, the users will have to log in to Resy or create a Resy profile.

Once your Resy availability is on Google, you will see a "Reserve a Table" button in the Google panel and when you click that button, you will see Resy as the booking partnership:

 [[IMAGE: google-integration__img03]][[IMAGE: google-integration__img04]]

**Please note**: If you see either another booking system or Resy and another booking system listed as the booking partnership, we suggest that you double check that you don't have any inventory available online with another booking system. If you don't have inventory online anywhere else then you should be all set. This process is automated and the other booking system will eventually go away once more guests book your Resy reservations through Google. 

If you have any further questions or would like to have your Google Integration disabled, please [contact Resy Support](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-contact-resy-support-Bk_JGP7Lu) for further assistance.
