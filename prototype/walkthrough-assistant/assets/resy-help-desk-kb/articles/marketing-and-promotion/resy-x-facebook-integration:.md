# Resy x Facebook Integration

## **Add the "Book Now" Button to your Facebook Page**

Guests can "Book Now" from your Facebook Business Profile page!

  

---

  

Here are some instructions on how to add a "Book Now" button to your page:

1. Click **+ Add a Button** below your page's cover photo.[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img01]]

2. Select **Book Now** from the menu.

[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img02]]

  
3. Select **Connect Another Tool** to view options.

[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img03]]

4. Select **Resy**.

[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img04]]

  

5. Select **Continue** to grant permissions.[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img05]]  

6. Sign in with your ResyOS credentials

[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img06]]  

  
7. Enter all accounts you would like to link and then select **Continue**.

[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img07]]

  
8. You're all set!

[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img08]]

  
Once the button has been created, you can test it by:

1. Hovering over your button.
2. Select "Test Button".

You can also edit or delete the “Book Now” button here.

[[IMAGE: resy-x-facebook-integration:__img09]]

  
To add a “Book Now” button to your Instagram profile, [click here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/resy-x-instagram-integration-BJsrZPmLd).
