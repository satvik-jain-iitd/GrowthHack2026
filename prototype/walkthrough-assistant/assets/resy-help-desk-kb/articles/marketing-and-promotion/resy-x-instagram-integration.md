# Resy x Instagram Integration

By integrating your Instagram profile with Resy, guests can directly book a reservation with the“**Reserve**” button on your profile.

**Please note:**

* Integration is only available on **Business Instagram Profiles**.
* Only 1 restaurant location can be connected to a single Instagram account.
* Make sure you set your profile to Public. Private accounts can't switch to Business Accounts.
* With an Instagram business account, you'll get access to new [business features](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/1714412405508502?helpref=faq_content) and [Instagram Insights](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/788388387972460?helpref=faq_content).

  

**In this article:**

* Setting up the “Reserve” Button
* How to Set up an Instagram Business Account
* Related Articles

### 

### **Setting up the “Reserve” Button**

1. Go to your Instagram Profile > **Edit Profile**.
2. Scroll down and tap Action Buttons > **Reserve** > **Resy**.
3. Sign in with your Resy OS email and password.
4. Select the Resy OS restaurant to connect.

**Please note:**If you have access to more than 1 restaurant, you'll see all restaurants.

1. Tap **Continue as ____**.
2. On the Confirm Settings page, tap **Continue**.
3. Tap **Active** on Profile.
4. Select the button next to **Reserve**.
5. Tap **Done** > **Done**  to complete and see the “Reserve” button on your profile.

## 

### **How to Set up an Instagram Business Account**

1. Go to your profile and tap the 3 horizontal lines in the top-right corner.
2. Tap **Settings** > **Account**.
3. Select **Switch to Professional Account**> **Continue**.
4. Choose a Category for your business > Select **Business** > **Next**.
5. Review your business's contact information, make any changes, and tap **Next**.
6. If you have a Facebook account, select the Facebook Page account you'd like to associate with your Business Profile on Instagram.
