# Reports in the ResyOS App

The ResyOS app provides reports on Reservations, Spread of Covers and Close of Shift. You can simply view these reports, print or email a report to your distribution list from directly within the report. 

**In this article:**

* Access ResyOS Report
* ResyOS Reports
	+ Reservation Report
	+ Spread of Covers
	+ Close of Shift
* Print/Email ResyOS Reports

---

  

### **Access ResyOS Reports**

In the ResyOS app, 

1. Click your venues name to view the menu.
2. Click '**Reports**'.
3. Click '**Select**' next to '**Report****Type**'.
4. Select the '**Report****Type**' and '**Service****Period**'.
5. Click '**Generate**'.

## ResyOS Reports

### 

### **Reservations Report**

The Reservation tab is a list of all upcoming reservations for the date and service period you selected. It includes the guests history of visits and guest/visit notes.  

[[IMAGE: resyos-apps-reports__img02]]

### 

### **Spread of Covers**

The number of covers for each 15 minute increment in the selected service period. 

[[IMAGE: resyos-apps-reports__img03]]

### 

### **Close Of Shift**

An overview of all guest activity in the select service period. 

[[IMAGE: resyos-apps-reports__img04]]

  

### **Printing and Emailing Reports in ResyOS**

To print a report in ResyOS,

1. On the report, click Choose Printer
2. Select your printer.
3. Click the printer button at the top of the report.

To email a report from ResyOS to your distribution list, 

1. On the report, click the blue button next to the printer button.
