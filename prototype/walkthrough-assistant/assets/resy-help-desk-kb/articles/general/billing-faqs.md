# Billing FAQs

# **Billing Frequently Asked Questions**

### **What are accepted payment methods?**

**Credit card/ACH**: The most convenient way to make a payment is by utilizing the RESY Billing Portal. You’ll be able to view all outstanding invoices and add a payment method (credit card/ACH). 

Note: If you have multiple accounts, you’ll be able to toggle between them if the same email address is tied to all of the accounts in our system. If you receive a message saying that your email does not exist, please reply to this email and request that your email be added.

### **Why can’t I pay by check?**

Effective 6/1/19, we no longer accept checks as a form of payment. We unfortunately do not have the resources to manually process checks for all of our partners each month. It is standard practice for SaaS businesses to require electronic payment as it allows a seamless communication flow and more accurate record-keeping.

### **How do I enroll in autopay?**

All accounts are automatically enrolled in auto-pay.

### **How do I access the Resy Billing Portal?**

Resy is transitioning to a new Billing Portal. The current Billing Portal is currently not accessible to Restaurant Partners. Please check back in December.

### H**ow do I update the bill to contact on my account?**

Please reach out to [billing@resy.com](mailto:billing@resy.com) to update the bill to contact on file.

### **Does Resy offer Net 30 Terms?**

No, invoices are due upon receipt and are sent out on a rolling basis. 

### **My account has been suspended. How do I reactivate service?**

To reactivate service, you’ll need to pay your outstanding balance owed. Please email [billing@resy.com](mailto:billing@resy.com) to have the payment on file processed. 

### **How do I cancel my service with Resy?**

Email our Support team at [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com) for information and assistance with cancellation requests. If you have an account manager, please reach out to them directly.

### **What are Resy billing hours of operation?**

Resy billing is open Monday – Friday 9:00 am – 6:00 pm EST. For help after 6:00 pm, please reach out to the Resy support team. We are closed in observance of all major holidays.

Please contact [Billing@Resy.com](mailto:Billing@Resy.com) with any additional billing questions that were not answered above.

### **How do I reach Resy Support?**

If you need assistance with issues outside of billing, please reach out to our support team who are available daily from 8:00 am - 2:00 am EST via chat and email. 

You can reach them by doing any of the following:

* tap the Venue Name in the upper left corner of the ResyOS app > Support
* via the support chat found at [helpdesk.resy.com](http://helpdesk.resy.com)
* via the support chat found at [os.resy.com/portal](http://os.resy.com/portal)
* via email at [resysupport@resy.com.](mailto:resysupport@resy.com.)
