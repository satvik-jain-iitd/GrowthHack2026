# POS Integrations: Toast

**In this article:** Integrating Toast POS with Resy  
**Please note:** To qualify for POS integration, restaurants are required to be on our Platform or Platform 360 package. This integration is only available to US restaurants.

---

### **Integrating Toast with Resy:**

1. Log in to your Toast account
2. Navigate to ***I******ntegrations > Integrations management > Browse & purchase integrations.***Enter **Resy** in the **Search** field and select ***Add Now***
3. Review the Terms & Conditions and hit **Confirm**
4. Navigate to ***Integrations*** in Toast Web and select ***Configure Integrations***. Select ***Settings***(shown as a gear icon)  
**[[IMAGE: pos-integrations-toast__img01]]**
5. Enter in your restaurant’s Resy Venue ID in the Location ID box
	* To find your Venue ID, a Resy Admin should log in to their ResyOS Dashboard at <https://os.resy.com/portal/login>
	* Click on **Venue and POS Settings**
	* Copy the Venue ID and paste it into the Toast Location ID field
6. Click **Apply**

*After completing the steps above, you**’**ll be able to leverage the*[*POS features*](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/pos-integrations-HkUQN3s5u)*within Resy**powered by the integration**.*

If you’re interested in setting up real-time course status updates, please [click this link](https://resy.chilipiper.com/round-robin/resy-real-time-course-status) to schedule a time to enable [Real-Time Course Status Updates](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/pos-integration-automatic-course-status-BkqVQL0n) (for accounts with active integrations only).

*For any additional questions, please reach out to your account manager or Resy Restaurant Support (resysupport@resy.com).*
