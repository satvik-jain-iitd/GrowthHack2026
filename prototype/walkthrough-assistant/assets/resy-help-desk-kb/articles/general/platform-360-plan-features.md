# Platform 360 Plan Features

Below is an overview of the features that are included in our Platform 360 plan. This plan is for restaurant partners who are looking for the ability to do customizable guest messaging and post-meal guest surveys. You will also have access to making desktop reservations and POS Integration. 

**In this article**:

* Platform 360 Plan Features

---

  

## Platform 360 Plan Features

  

### Surveys

You will have access to our Surveys feature which are private customizable post-meal questions that allow guests to review their experience at your restaurant. The reviews are not public facing, only you and your staff will have the ability to view the reviews left by guests. Click [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/resy-surveys-S1qQMwX8u) for more information about Surveys. 

  

---

### 

### Exclusive Groups

Exclusive Booking allows you to allocate online reservations to a select group of guests. One or more of your VIP, Concierge, and Guest tags can be used to create an online channel where only guests with those tags will be able to see those reservations. Click [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-use-exclusive-online-booking-channels-BkihvQIO) for more information about Exclusive Groups. 

  

---

### 

### Dashboard Booking

You can book reservations directly on the Resy Dashboard, allowing your reservationist to provide a better guest experience and toggle between different venues more efficiently. In addition, the booking process was built to be as flexible as your reservationist, so the steps to book a reservation can be completed in any order they see fit. Click [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/desktop-reservation-flow-SJEDlJNn2) for more information on booking from your Dashboard. 

  

---

### 

### Email Customizations

Make updates to the emails guests receive when they book at your restaurant. Edit language for the Confirmation, Cancellation, and Reservation Change emails. Click [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/customizing-email-content-H1u6bDQ8d) for more information on guest email customizations. 

  

---

### 

### Text Message Customizations

Customize the language and timing for text messages guests receive when they book at your restaurant. Click [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/customizing-text-message-content-HJP6WwQLO) for more information customizing text messages. 

  

---

### 

### Resy Prepay

Use PrePay when someone calls to buy their friend a bottle of wine or cover the whole meal for one of the guests in your restaurant. PrePay sends them a secure payment link via text to pay and the funds are deposited in your account. Prepay can also be used as a contactless payment method. Guests can enter their own credit card information securely and your team can charge their card at the end of their experience once the final bill has been determined. Click [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-use-resy-prepay-feature-in-resyos-rJTzMvXLu) for more information on Resy Prepay.

### POS Integration

Integrate Resy and POS to understand your guest’s spending habits, improve operational efficiencies at the door, and receive actionable reporting about your business. Chick [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/pos-integrations-HkUQN3s5u) for more information on compatible POS systems and what integrating your POS with Resy offers.
