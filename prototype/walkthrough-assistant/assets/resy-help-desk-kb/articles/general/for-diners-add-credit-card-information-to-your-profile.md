# For Diners: Manage Credit Card Information on Your Profile

You may need to add your credit card information to your Resy profile. Follow the instructions below to make this update. 

**In this article:**

* How to add a Credit Card to your profile on Resy.com
* How to add a Credit Card to your profile in the Resy App

  

---

## 

## Resy.com

1. On Resy.com, click the silhouette on the upper-right corner.
2. Click “Payment Methods”.

[[IMAGE: for-diners-add-credit-card-information-to-your-profile__img01]]

3. From here, you have the following options:

* “Add Credit Card” to add a new credit card to your profile.
* “Make Default” if you have multiple credit cards on your profile you can click this to choose which one should be the default.
* “Remove” to delete a credit card from your profile.

[[IMAGE: for-diners-add-credit-card-information-to-your-profile__img02]]

  

## Resy App

1. In the Resy App, tap the silhouette icon on the upper-left corner.

  

## [[IMAGE: for-diners-add-credit-card-information-to-your-profile__img03]]2. Tap the gear icon on the upper-right corner.

[[IMAGE: for-diners-add-credit-card-information-to-your-profile__img04]]

3. Tap "Payment Methods".  
  

[[IMAGE: for-diners-add-credit-card-information-to-your-profile__img05]]

  

4. From there you have the follow options: * Tap “Add Card” to add a new credit card.
* Tap the 3 dots next to the card to “Make Primary Card” if you have multiple credit cards on file or “Remove” to delete a credit card.
