# Resy OS Tags Functionality

Resy OS Tags optimizes your restaurant operations. This article will better explain the behavior and functionality of Guest Tags, Visit Tags, and the share-ability if your venue is a part of a group.   

  

**In this article** :

* Resy OS Tags Overview
* Specifics of Resy OS Tags
* Share-ability of Tags

---

### 

### **Resy OS Tags Overview:**

* Resy OS Tags plays a huge role in optimizing restaurant operations.
* Each Tag is classified by various categories and sub-categories, and based on the category, the Tag has a different behavior.
	+ ***Please note** this is especially important with the way the tag can be shared across venue groups.*

### **Specifics of Resy OS Tags:**

* Depending on the category, Resy has default sharing capabilities.
	+ ***Please note****: Resy is unable to customize sharing options at this time.*
* All Tags are labeled either as a Guest Tag or a Visit Tag.
* A Guest Tag exists at the diner-level profile and will follow the guest throughout their time with Resy, regardless of where they dine.
* A Visit Tag exists at the reservation level and is specific to the individual instance the guest dines at a restaurant.
* For both Guest and Visit Tags, the following categories exist: **Food and Beverage (F&B), Hospitality, and General**.
* **For the above categories, the following sub-categories exist**:
	+ Food and Beverage Sub-Category:
		- Allergy/Dietary Restrictions – Shareable
		- Dietary Preferences – Shareable
	+ Hospitality Sub-Category:
		- Identification – Shareable
		- Relationship – Venue Only
		- Concierge – Venue Only
	+ General Sub-category – Venue Only

### **Share-ability of Tags:**

* The logic for sharing Guest Tags can be thought of as follows:
	+ All Food and Beverage Guest Tags are automatically shared across the entirety of the venue group.
	+ Hospitality Guest Tags are shared across venue groups depending on the sub-category.
		- Identification Tags are shared across venue groups.
		- Relationship Tags are one venue only.
		- Concierge Tags are one venue only.

  

Here is another way to look at what falls under Guest Tags:

| **Guest Tags** | **Visit Tags** |
| --- | --- |
| Food & Beverage -sharedAllergy/ Restrictions- shared Dietary preferences -shared | Food & Beverage-shared Allergy/ Restrictions- shared Dietary preferences –shared |
| HospitalityIdentification -shared Relationship Concierge | HospitalityReason for visit Visit Needs |
| General | General |

  

***Please note****: For Visit Tags, only the Food & Beverage category tags are shareable across venue groups.*
