# American Express® Reward Card FAQ

We recently launched a Resy-branded American Express® Reward Card that gives diners the opportunity to use when dining out at Resy restaurants. The following tips can be communicated to diners if they are looking to use the Reward Card as a form of payment while dining out.

**How do I use my American Express Reward Card at a restaurant?**

1. Know your Reward Card balance before you dine. You can check your Card balance online for free at [amexrewardcard.com](http://www.amexrewardcard.com) or by calling 1-833-249-6098.
2. Present your Card like any other credit card after dining, sign the receipt, and go!
3. To use your Reward Card along with another form of payment such as cash or credit card, let the restaurant know you have more than one payment type and how much you want to apply to each.

**Please note**: Not all merchants allow split payment transactions and some transaction types require a hold of up to 20% above the purchase amount. When dining out, restaurants typically do this to account for the tip. See [amexrewardcard.com/cardholder-agreement](https://www.amexrewardcard.com/cardholder-agreement) for more information.
