# How to Close Days in Dashboard

#### Close a specific day or shift in your Calendar under Shift Settings

1. Log in to [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal) using Chrome.
2. Select “Service" from the left-hand menu.
3. Click “Calendar”.
4. Navigate and tap the specific day.
5. Select “Close Shift or Day”.
6. Choose “All Day” or a specific shift.
7. Enter a note (optional).
8. Tap the blue “Close” button when done.

[[IMAGE: how-to-close-days-in-dashboard__img01]]  

#### To Reopen Close or Shift

Tap the closed day in your Calendar and tap "Open Day".
