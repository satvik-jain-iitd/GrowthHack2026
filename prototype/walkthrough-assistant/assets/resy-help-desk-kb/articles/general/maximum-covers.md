# Maximum Covers

The Maximum Covers feature allows you to set a limit to the number of bookable covers for an entire shift by automatically disabling online reservations once the limit has been met. A pop-up warning will also appear in Resy OS when you make in-house reservations or changes that will cause you to go over the set limit.

## Setting Your Maximum Covers Limit

To establish your Maximum Covers for your shifts:

1. Go to your [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues).
2. Click “Service” from the left-hand menu.
3. Click “Shift Settings” from the drop-down to access your recurring base shifts.
4. Click the three dots on the shift you want to update.
5. Select “Edit Shift”.
6. Go to “Step 2: Service Settings” and scroll to “Maximum Covers”.
7. Enter the maximum numbers of guests you can accommodate for the entire shift.
8. Click “Update Shift”.
9. This shift will now automatically take online availability “Offline” once your cover limit has been met. You can return to the Maximum Covers setting and adjust the cover limit for your shift at any time.

### 

## Disabling the Maximum Covers

1. Go to your [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues).
2. Click “Service".
3. Click the “Shift Settings” from the drop-down to access your recurring base shifts.
4. Click the three dots on the shift you want to update.
5. Select “Edit Shift".
6. Go to “Step 2: Service Settings” and select “Maximum Covers".
7. Delete the number that is entered as the maximum cover limit.
8. Click “Update Shift” and save in the bottom-right corner.

## Maximum Covers Pop-up Warnings in Resy OS

Now that your maximum covers limit has been set, you'll receive pop-up warnings in the Resy OS app to notify you and your team when in-house actions cause you to exceed the cover limit you've set for your shift. The pop-up is just a warning and you'll be given the option to proceed with overbooking the maximum covers if you choose to.

**In-house actions will trigger a cover limit pop-up warning:**

* **Booking a reservation**-When you attempt to book a reservation once you've met your cover limit, the following pop-up warning will appear before allowing you to continue. The warning will trigger when booking from the booking modal and directly from the timeline view.

*“Booking this reservation will go over the maximum cover limit set for this shift. Are you sure you want to book this?”*

* **Changing a reservation**-When changing the time or party size of a reservation causes you to exceed your maximum cover limit, the following pop-up warning will appear before allowing you to continue.

*"Changing this reservation will go over the maximum cover limit set for this shift. Are you sure you want to make this change?"*

* **Seating a party**- When seating a reservation, a walk-in or waitlist party on the floor plan causes you to exceed your maximum cover limit, the following pop-up warning will appear before allowing you to continue seating the reservation.

"Seating this reservation will go over the maximum cover limit for this shift. Are you sure you want to continue?"

* **Sending “Table Ready Text” in the waitlist flow**- When updating the status of a waitlist reservation to "Wait: table ready", the following pop-up warning will appear before sending the table ready notification if the waitlist party would cause you to exceed your maximum cover limit.

*"Seating this waitlist party will go over the maximum cover limit set for this shift. Are you sure you want to update this status to Table Ready?"*

## Resy OS Maximum Covers Indicators

**Spread of Covers View**

* All timestamps and covers will be highlighted in red when the maximum cover limit is exceeded.
* Tapping the spread of covers bar to the open view will show the maximum cover limit you have set for the shift on the left-hand side and the total covers for each 15-min interval you can expect to have in the restaurant based on your current reservations and seated guests.
* If no maximum cover limit is set in web dashboard for the shift, the maximum cover limit will not show in the spread of covers view in either the closed or open states.

## **ResyOS Booking Modal**

From the booking modal, available tables that would cause you to exceed your maximum cover limit will be indicated with a red check mark. As noted, if you attempt to book a table that would cause you to exceed the cover limit, you'll also receive a pop-up warning. The visual indicator in the booking modal can help you more quickly identify the appropriate tables to offer guests when booking in-house ahead of the secondary pop-up warning.
