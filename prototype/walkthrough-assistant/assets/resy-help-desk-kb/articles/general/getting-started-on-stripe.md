# Getting Started on Stripe

To help your restaurant get started using Stripe, this article provides step-by-step instructions on how to set up your Stripe account. 

**Please note:** Prior to filling out any information, your account **must be created by someone authorized to sign in on your organization’s behalf.**

Setting up your Stripe account: 

1. Sign into the[ResyOS Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/login). Click **"Venue"** from the left-hand menu options. 

2. Click **"Transaction Settings"**.

  

[[IMAGE: getting-started-on-stripe__img01]]

  

3. Click **"Start Selling with Resy"** to be redirected to the Stripe Dashboard.

  

[[IMAGE: getting-started-on-stripe__img02]]

**Please note:** The ResyOS Dashboard image (pictured above) can vary depending on your restaurant’s location, if you’ve previously submitted banking information, or are a newly onboarded Resy restaurant partner. 

4. Click "Banking Info" to be redirected to the Stripe login page. Stripe will ask you to create an account with a unique log in and password. **Please keep a record of the log in and password created and do not share these credentials.**

5. Once you have created your Stripe account, you'll be asked to fill out information about your restaurant, such as your **business details, banking information, public details, payout details, and ownership information.** After filling out the required information, please click "Submit". 

6. Click “Set up Sales Tax" to view and edit your Tax Rate settings. If you’re transacting with Resy, you’ll need to add Prepaid Tax Type that matches your local or state %.

What does the transaction status in the Resy OS Dashboard mean? 

Transaction Status (also known as status badges) provides a quick way to understand the status of your Stripe account. You can view the status of your Stripe account in both the Stripe dashboard and Resy OS Dashboard. Your venue can transact and accept payments via Stripe if your account is in a complete status. Your venue may experience disruptions in transacting and accepting payments via Stripe if your account is in an 'enabled' or 'restricted soon' status. 

**Please note:** Your venue will**not**be able to transact or process payments through Stripe if your status says**"****Restricted"**. Click **"****Stripe Dashboard"** (see first image below) to be automatically redirected to Stripe to finish setting up your account from where you previously left off. To learn more about each status [visit this article](https://stripe.com/docs/connect/dashboard)provided by Stripe. The second image below shows that your Stripe account has been completed and you're ready to begin transacting and accepting payments on Stripe.
