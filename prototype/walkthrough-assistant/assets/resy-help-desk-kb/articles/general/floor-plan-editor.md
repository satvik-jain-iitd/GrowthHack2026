# ResyOS Floor Plan Editor

Create new floor plans and edit your existing floor plans from your Resy OS app on the iPad. 

## **In this article**

* How to Create, Edit, and Copy a Floor Plan
* How to Create a New Table
* How to Edit a Table
* How to Create and Edit Table Combinations
* How to Update a Floor Plan for a Single Day Edit, Holiday, or Event
* How to Print a Floor Plan on an iPad

  

### **How to Create, Edit, and Copy a Floor Plan**

1. Tap the restaurant name in the top-left corner to reveal the “Venue Settings” dropdown menu.
2. Select “Edit Floor Plan”.
	* **Please note:****If you do not see this option in the dropdown menu in Resy OS, you can adjust user permissions to allow access in the dashboard.**
3. Tap on the floor plan name at the top of “Edit Floor Plan” to open the “Manage Floor Plans” window. From there, you can do the following:
* **Build a new floor plan from scratch**: Tap the "+" icon.
* **Edit the name of an existing floor plan**: Select any of your existing floor plans and tap the Pen and Paper icon to rename.
* **Copy a floor plan**: Select the floor plan and tap the pages icon for a quick way to make a copy of an existing floor plan and make minor edits for holidays or events.

### **How to Create a New Table**

1. Tap the white shapes at the bottom of the floor plan to create a new table. The new table will appear directly on the floor plan and can be dragged to the desired location and resized by pinching or expanding.
2. The “Table Details” side panel will be open for you to enter the following details:
* **Table ID:**The Table ID must be unique, as this is how reservations are assigned. For example, you can only have one table 20 on your floor plan. Tap the Table ID field to edit.
* **Table Type:**The Table Type should reflect the seating type of the table. For example, Dining Room, Bar, Patio, or High Top are standard table types.
* **Min/Max Seats:**Input the minimum and maximum capacity for the party size that will be allowed to book and be assigned to this table.

**Please note:**

* *If you change the Table ID (Table Number) on existing tables with reservations, the existing reservations will become unassigned. You will then need to re-assign these reservations. If you unknowingly unassigned reservations, you can revert the Table ID back to the original ID and your reservations will automatically re-assign to the original assignment.*
* **All new tables that are created will be set to in-house by default.****You will then need to log in to the online Resy Dashboard and adjust your service settings to update your table availability to the desired status for the shift.**
* **You have to add your floor plan to the shift for it to be active.****To activate your new floor plan, you will need to log in to the Resy dashboard and toggle on the floor plan under the desired shift.**
* *You can use the Table Type as the Reservation Display Name or create custom policies und***er***your shift settings to reflect a different reservation display name.*

### **How to Edit Existing Tables**

1. Tap the floor plan name in the top center of the screen and select the floor plan you want to edit.
2. Tap on a table on the floor plan to launch the “Table Details” in the left side panel. Edit Table ID, Table Type, and Min/Max Seats.
3. Tap and drag tables to new locations. Tap and drag blue dots to resize the shape. Use the orange dot to rotate the shape.
4. Select “Save” in the bottom-right corner to complete floor plan edits.

### 

### **How to Create and Edit Table Combinations**

1. Click on the floor plan you’d like to make edits to listed under “Floor Plan Name”.
2. Tap the “Combinations” tab at the top of the “Table Details” to see a list of current combinations and tap an existing combination on the list to edit the Table Details.
3. Tap “Create Combo” at the bottom of the panel to add a new combination.
4. Select a new combination by tapping all the tables on the floor plan you'd like to combine and adding the Table Type, and Min/Max Seats in the left side panel.
5. Select “Done” to complete the combination.
6. Select “Save” in the bottom-right corner to complete floor plan edits.

### **How to Update a Floor Plan for a Single Day Edit, Holiday, or Event**

1. Start by accessing the “Edit Floor Plan” tool from the iPad dropdown menu.
2. Tap the venue name in the top-left corner.
3. Select “Edit Floor Plan”.
4. Tap the floor plan name in the top middle of the screen and select “Manage Floor Plans”.
5. Tap the floor plan you wish to use.
6. Tap the two-page icon to duplicate the floor plan.
7. Enter the name of the new floor plan.
8. Click “X” to exit out of the “Manage Floor Plan” view.
9. Tap the floor plan name in the top center of the screen and select the newly named floor plan.
10. Make your floor plan edits.
11. Click “Save”.

After creating the new floor plan, you will need to activate it in the dedicated shift or event. Here are some directions on [how to activate your Floor Plan for a specific day](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-activate-floor-plan-and-edit-table-availability-for-a-single-day-H1Q3Wv7IO). 

### **How to Print a Floor Plan on an iPad**

 

1. Tap the restaurant name in the top-left corner to reveal the “Venue Settings” dropdown menu.
2. Select “Edit Floor Plan”.
	* **Please note: If you do not see this option in the dropdown menu in Resy OS, you     can adjust user permissions to allow access in the dashboard.**
3. Tap the desired floor plan name in the top middle of the screen.
4. Tap on the Printer icon in the top-right corner of the floor plan editor, which will open Printer Options.
5. Select the printer and adjust the printer settings as needed.
6. Tap “Print” in the top-right corner of the screen.

 

**A few things to keep in mind:**

* The system will default to black and white printing with a white background and black tables. An example of this is below.
* Seating capacity dots will change to numbers to signify the minimum and maximum seating capacity of each table.
* The floor plan will be scaled to fit to page.
* This feature is not available on an iPhone.
