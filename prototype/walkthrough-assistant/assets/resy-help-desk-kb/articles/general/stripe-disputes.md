# Stripe Disputes

A dispute occurs when a guest questions a charge made at your restaurant with their card issuer. There is a dispute resolution process in which you can respond and submit evidence to support the charge the customer is disputing to demonstrate the payment was valid. If the dispute is resolved in your favor, the disputed amount will be returned to your account. 

**Please note**: The dispute process can take 2-3 months to complete. 

Here are some best practices that could help avoid disputes:

1. Make sure you and your team **ONLY** charge the guest a cancellation fee if they have in fact been **CONFIRMED** as a no-show. 

2. Set up a email inbox for your restaurant that is managed by your team to actively monitor when a guest disputes a charge, instead of going through their card bank issuer. 

3. Add a double confirmation message to specific tables or all reservations. This pop-up message can contain important information and reiterate your cancellation policy that you would like guests to confirm they have read before they can complete booking their reservation. [Click here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/double-confirmation-message-B1Km8K46F) for step-by-step directions on how to set up a double confirmation message. 

4. Add a detailed description of your cancellation policy to your confirmation and pro-tip text messaging. 

What is a dispute? 

A dispute occurs when a cardholder questions your payment with their card issuer. You can learn more about how disputes work with Stripe[here](https://stripe.com/docs/disputes).

Where can I locate a dispute? 

You can locate disputes via the[Stripe Dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com/test/disputes) under the ‘Payments’ section. 

**Please note:** If you decide to proceed with filing a dispute, Stripe provides a guided process within the Stripe Dashboard.

 How does a dispute work? 

When a guest disputes a charge with their bank to their payment account, Stripe will: 

1. Notify you of the dispute in the Stripe Dashboard and via email.
2. Debit the disputed amount, plus a dispute fee, from your Stripe account until the dispute is resolved.
3. Provide you with an explanation of the dispute and access to the account owner’s claim to their bank.
4. Walk you through the process to submit documentation supporting the charge from your restaurant that the customer is disputing.
