# Resy + Claude Integration

Resy is launching an integration with Claude to drive incremental covers for your restaurant with zero added operational effort.

 

---

  

**What is it?**  
  
We’re securely surfacing your real-time Resy availability directly within the Claude channels. Diners can discover your restaurant availability on Claude, while the reservation flows directly into your Resy OS, keeping Resy as the system of record. Diners will be linked out to Resy channels to complete the booking flow after selecting a booking brick in Claude. 

 

---

  

**Key benefits:**

* **Increased visibility:** Reach diners searching for restaurants on Claude
* **Incremental bookings:** Capture more covers through a new high-visibility channel
* **No extra work:** Seamless, automated integration. No need to manage a separate system
* **Centralized operations:** All bookings appear in Resy OS

**What types of reservations and experiences are supported through Claude?**

All unpaid and paid reservations will be supported through this integration. No events or gated inventory will appear on Claude at this time. 

**Does Claude maintain diner data or use it for targeted results?**

No sensitive personal data is shared with Claude.  
  

---

  
**How to opt-out**

This integration is designed to drive growth for your business. If you prefer not to have diners book at your venue through Claude, please contact Resy Support via chat in the Resy OS app/Dashboard or at resysupport@resy.com.
