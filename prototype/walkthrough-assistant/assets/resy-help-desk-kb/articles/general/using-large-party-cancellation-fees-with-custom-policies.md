# Using Large Party Cancellation Fees with Custom Policies

**What is a Large Party Cancellation Fee?**

Large Party Cancellation Fees provide our restaurant partners with more flexibility to set cancellation policies by party size instead of per shift or per table.  

**What's included in this version?**

A new option under Cancellation Fee in Step 4 of Shift Settings to set up a Large Party Cancellation Fee Policy on the shift.  

**If I have custom policies without cancellation policies in Step 5 of the Shift Settings, can I still add a Large Party Cancellation Fee in Step 4?**

Yes, but please note that the Large Party Cancellation Fee will not automatically update your Custom Policies in Step 5. You will need to manually turn on a cancellation policy in Step 5 if these tables should have a cancellation fee. 

**Please note**: The cancellation policy will only apply if the existing party size settings match. 

 

**Can I turn on a Large Party Cancellation Fee in Step 4 if I have custom policies with cancellation fees applied in Step 5?**

No, turning on a Large Party Cancellation Fee will override the cancellation policies set in Step 5.  

 [[IMAGE: using-large-party-cancellation-fees-with-custom-policies__img01]]

**Can I turn on a Large Party Cancellation Fee in Step 4 if I have custom policies with prepaid reservations in Step 5?**

Yes, Large Party Cancellation Fees will not override any custom prepaid reservation policies set in Step 5. 

 

**Should I use a Large Party Cancellation Fee in Step 4 of my shift settings if I need different cancellation policies for different tables?**

No, Large Party Cancellation Fees would not be a good fit for this. Instead, you should set a standard cancellation policy in Step 4 and customize the policies for specific tables in Step 5. 

 

**If I add a Large Party Cancellation Fee or change my existing cancellation fee price, how are these fees processed for guests who booked before the change?**

As of August 2022, all reservations that were booked before the cancellation policy change will have the **new** policy applied automatically,**but you won't be able to charge the guest for the new policy given the guest didn't agree to that fee upon booking the initial reservation.** Instead, the restaurant will see an error message on Resy OS that states, "This action cannot be completed because you've changed your cancellation policy since the guest initially booked this reservation. Resy will attempt to process the original cancellation fee that the guest agreed upon within 48 hours. Please note successful processing is subject to the card member's issuing bank's approval."  

**Please note**: The charge for the original cancellation fee will be manually charged by a member of the Resy Billing Team pending the card member's issuing bank's approval.
