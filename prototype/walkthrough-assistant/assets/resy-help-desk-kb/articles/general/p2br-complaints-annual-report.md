# P2BR Complaints Annual Report

As a provider of an online intermediation services platform, we publish reports on the effectiveness of our internal complaints handling processes annually in accordance with the European Platform to Business Regulation (P2BR). This reporting can be found here.

  

## **2022 Results**

* Total P2BR complaints received = 11
* Main types of P2BR complaints and number of complaints received each:
1. P2BR Issues = 0
2. Technology Issues = 11
3. Account Issues = 0
* Average time needed to process the complaints = 37.7 days
* Aggregated information regarding the outcome of the complaints:
	1. All 11 complaints (100%) from 2022 were resolved.
