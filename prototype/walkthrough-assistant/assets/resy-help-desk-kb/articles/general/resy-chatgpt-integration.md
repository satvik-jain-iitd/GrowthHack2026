# Resy + ChatGPT Integration

Resy is launching an integration with ChatGPT to drive incremental covers for your restaurant with zero added operational effort.  
  

---

  
**What is it?**  
  
We’re securely surfacing your real-time Resy availability directly within the ChatGPT channels. Diners can discover your restaurant and book via Resy widget on ChatGPT, while the reservation flows directly into your Resy OS, keeping Resy as the system of record. 

---

  
**Key benefits:**

* **Increased visibility:** Reach diners searching for restaurants on ChatGPT
* **Incremental bookings:** Capture more covers through a new high-visibility channel
* **No extra work:** Seamless, automated integration. No need to manage a separate system
* **Centralized operations:** All bookings appear in Resy OS

**What types of reservations and experiences are supported through ChatGPT?**

All unpaid and paid reservations will be supported through this integration. No events or gated inventory will appear on ChatGPT at this time.   
  

---

  
**How to opt-out**

This integration is designed to drive growth for your business. If you prefer not to have diners book at your venue through ChatGPT, please contact Resy Support via chat in the Resy OS app/Dashboard or at resysupport@resy.com.
