# For Diners: Change or Cancel a Reservation

You may need to make updates to your reservation. Review the steps below to see how to change or cancel your booking.  

In this Article:

* How to change or cancel a reservation on Resy.com
* How to change or cancel a reservation in the Resy App

 

---

## 

## Resy.com

1. On Resy.com, click the silhouette icon in the upper-right corner.
2. Click “Reservations”.

[[IMAGE: for-diners-change-or-cancel-a-reservation__img01]]

  

3. Under “Upcoming Reservations”, click “Manage Reservation” next to the reservation you want to change or cancel. 

[[IMAGE: for-diners-change-or-cancel-a-reservation__img02]]4. Click “Change Reservation” to make updates to the current reservations (i.e., the date, time, or party size). Click “Cancel” if you would like to completely cancel the booking. 

**Please *note****: Be sure to review the details when cancelling a reservation to see if any cancellation fee charges apply.*

*[[IMAGE: for-diners-change-or-cancel-a-reservation__img03]]*

  

## Resy App

1. In the Resy app, click the silhouette icon in the upper-left corner.

[[IMAGE: for-diners-change-or-cancel-a-reservation__img04]]

2.  Click the 3 dots next to the reservation you need to change or cancel.

[[IMAGE: for-diners-change-or-cancel-a-reservation__img05]]

  

3. Click “Change Reservation” to make updates to the current reservations (i.e., the date, time, or party size). Click “Cancel Reservation” if you would like to completely cancel the booking. **Please note**: Be sure to review the details when cancelling a reservation to see if any cancellation fee charges apply.

[[IMAGE: for-diners-change-or-cancel-a-reservation__img06]]
