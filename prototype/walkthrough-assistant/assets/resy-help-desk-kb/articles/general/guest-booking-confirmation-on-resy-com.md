# How Guests can Add Important Details to their Reservation

When a guest books a reservation via the Resy App or [Resy.com](//Resy.com), guests have the ability to let the restaurant know any important details regarding their upcoming reservation. 

**In this article:**

* What'sthe occasion?
* Any allergies or restrictions?
* Any special requests?
* Invite Your Party

---

  

## What's the occasion?

Guests have the option to select if they are celebrating a special occasion. If they select one of the options, the selected answer will appear on the guest's reservation profile as a Visit Tag. [[IMAGE: guest-booking-confirmation-on-resy-com__img01]]

## Any allergies or restrictions?

The guest will have the ability to list any allergies or dietary restrictions for themselves or the guests in their party. Any answers selected by the guest will appear as a Guest Tag on the guest's profile. If the guests adds a Dietary Note, the note will be added as a locked Guest Note on the guest's profile. 

[[IMAGE: guest-booking-confirmation-on-resy-com__img02]]

## Any special requests?

The guest can describe any special accommodations they may need for their reservation here. Special requests will be added as a Visit Note on the guest's reservation profile. [[IMAGE: guest-booking-confirmation-on-resy-com__img03]]

## Invite Your Party

Here, guests can invite members of their party to their reservation. This article explains how guests can invite people to their reservation, [How Hosts can Invite Guests to their Reservation](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/guests-can-invite-fellow-diners-to-their-reservation-party-SJmPvXUO). [[IMAGE: guest-booking-confirmation-on-resy-com__img04]]
