# Pacing by Floor Plan FAQ

### Resy OS now has the ability to configure pacing by different seating types to fit your restaurant's unique needs and to help provide more control and accuracy across all of your dining spaces.

  

### **What is Pacing by Floor Plan?**

The ability to specify pacing counts for each floor plan in ResyOS. Pacing refers to the number of covers you can accept per 15-minute time slot during a shift. 

  

### **Who will have access to Pacing by Floor Plan?**

All Resy restaurant partners have access to Pacing by Floor Plan. The feature will be enabled for all users. 

### **Can Pacing by Floor Plan be permission restricted?**

No, there is no user permission for setting pacing by floor plan. 

### **Why would a restaurant need to adjust pacing for specific floor plans?**

The feature allows restaurants to account for factors such as space constraints, server availability, and customer preference. 

### **How do I access Pacing by Floor Plan?**

Pacing by Floor Plan is now available in Step 2 of Shift Settings. 

1. From the left-hand menu > click "Service" > click "Shift Settings".
2. Click the three dots on the top-right corner of the shift > "Edit Shift".
3. In "Step 2 - Service Settings", navigate to “Pacing”.
4. Click “Customize Pacing by Floor Plan” beneath the current pacing settings.

 

### **Will users still have access to global pacing, which applies to all floor plans?**

Yes, settings are defaulted to global pacing settings. Clicking “**Save**” in the “**Customize Pacing by Floor Plan**” feature removes the default setting.  

  

### **How do I reset my pacing settings to apply to all floor plans?**

After setting floor plan specific pacing limits, you can always return to global pacing, which applies to all floor plans collectively. To do so, click "**Reset Pacing Settings**" in **Step 2 - Service Settings.**

### **Can I set different pacing values on each 15-minute time slot on any given floor plan?**

  
Yes, when editing pacing by floor plan, you can choose to set a value that applies to all time slots on that floor plan **or**you can click “Customize Intervals” next to each floor plan to select and adjust pacing by time slot. 

  
### **Can I set different pacing values for individual tables within each floor plan?**

  
No, the customized pacing values are determined by floor plan. Pacing values will apply to all tables within the selected floor plan. 

  
### **How does Pacing by Floor Plan impact my total pacing limits on a shift?**

  
Your total Pacing limit will be the sum of all values in the "Max Cover per 15 Mins" column in Step 2 of Shift Settings.  

  
### **Does the Spread of Covers report in the ResyOS app specify pacing limits by floor plan?**

  
No, pacing limits are not outlined by floor plan in the Spread of Covers report. However, the total pacing number will be an aggregate of all floor plan pacing limits.
