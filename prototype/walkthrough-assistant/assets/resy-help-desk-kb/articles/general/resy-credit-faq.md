# Resy Credit FAQ

The Resy Credit is a benefit offered by American Express® to eligible Card Members that rewards them with statement credits when they make qualifying purchases at restaurants that are live on Resy. These purchases may include in-person dining or pre-paid experiences booked through Resy and processed via the restaurant’s point-of-sale system or Resy Pay. This benefit provides value to Card Members while supporting Resy restaurant partners — at no additional cost to the restaurant.

## Resy Credit Breakdown by Card Type:

American Express offers Resy Credits across several Card products, each with a specified statement credit amount for Card Members to earn. Gold Card Members can earn up to $50 semi-annually in statement credits (for a total of up to $100 annually) for eligible dining purchases at U.S. Resy restaurants. Delta SkyMiles® Platinum Card Members, both Consumer and Business, can earn up to $120 annually in statement credits, provided as $10 per month on eligible purchases. Delta SkyMiles® Reserve Card Members, both Consumer and Business, can earn up to $240 annually in statement credits, provided as $20 per month on eligible purchases. Meanwhile, Personal Platinum Card® Members enjoy the highest benefit, with up to $400 annually in credits (up to $100 quarterly) for eligible dining purchases at Resy restaurants and other eligible Resy purchases. All benefits require enrollment and apply only to eligible Resy U.S. restaurant transactions.

## How the Resy Credit Benefits Our Restaurant Partners

### Drives Spending

The Resy Credit can encourage eligible Card Members to dine at restaurants live on Resy, increasing traffic and spend. Since the benefit launched, American Express Card Members with the Resy Credit have spent on average 25% more at U.S. Resy restaurants. Additionally, over 95% of U.S. Resy restaurants have seen at least one eligible Card Member earn a Resy Credit—demonstrating wide-reaching impact on in-restaurant purchasing behavior.  
  

### Improves Loyalty

Card Members may return to use their credits throughout the benefit period, fostering repeat engagement. Since launch, there has been a 10% increase in reservations at U.S. Resy restaurants made by Card Members enrolled in the Resy Credit, highlighting the program's ability to drive consistent, repeat bookings.

## What are eligible Resy purchases?

Eligible Resy purchases include purchases at U.S. restaurants that accept American Express and offer reservations on Resy.com and the Resy app, purchases made directly on Resy.com or in the Resy app, and purchases made via Resy Pay. 

Keep in mind: Restaurants must be live on Resy.com or the Resy app at the time of purchase to qualify for the statement credit. This status is subject to change at any time.

### When might a Resy purchase not be eligible for the Resy Credit?

#### Purchases that may not qualify for the Resy Credit include those:

1. Made at a restaurant inside another business (like a hotel or department store).
2. For non-dining items such as Resy-branded merchandise; Resy OS restaurant management software; Resy-branded American Express® Gift Cards; and gift cards purchased at restaurants.
3. Processed using a third-party system (e.g., mobile card readers or payment platforms).
4. Completed through food delivery apps or other third-party services.
