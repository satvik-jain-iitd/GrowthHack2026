# Resy OS Features Terms

Here you’ll find a list of Resy features and industry definitions.

  

---

  

### Types of Automated Messages

There are three types of automated text messages your guests can receive:

* **Reservation Confirmation:**Allows guests to confirm or cancel their reservations through SMS, saving you time from manual confirmations and encouraging timely cancellations to give you the best shot to rebook when guests’ plans change.
* **Day-of Reminder:**This offers an opportunity to send some extra information about the restaurant to guests just ahead of their reservation time, helps reduce late arrivals, and offers an opportunity for guests to reply directly to you with last-minute information that you may have otherwise not found out until they arrived.
* **Waitlist Messaging:**Automated texts for adding, confirming, and cancelling guests on the waitlist.

  

---

  

### Availability and Pacing

* **Pacing:** This is how many covers you have per 15-minute increment. We recommend keeping it lower during prime time.
* **Table access:**This varies by restaurant and party sizes you want to offer. Choose what tables are online, in-house, or walk-in only and when.

  

---

  

### User Permission Types

* **Update Venue and Banking Information**: Users can access the “Venue” section of the Resy Dashboard where they can edit online listing information, online pictures, and submit banking information for direct deposit.
* **Update Shifts**: Users can access “Shift Settings” in the “Service” section of the Resy Dashboard where they can create and edit recurring shifts.
* **Single-Day Edits**: Users can access “Calendar” in the “Service” section of the Resy Dashboard where they can make Single Day Edits to your availability and set up events.
* **Guest Communications**: Users can access the “Communications” section of the Resy Dashboard where they can turn on/off your automated SMS and customize language for automated SMS and emails if you're signed up for Platform 360 or above.
* **Manage Users**: Users can access the “Accounts” section of the Resy Dashboard where they can create new users, edit user permissions, and set up password protections.

### Basic Info

The “Venue” page lets guests know where the restaurant is located and how they can contact you.

* **Latitude/Longitude:** This determines where your pin will be dropped on a map for guests to find the restaurants location. So be sure it's accurate!.
* **Neighborhood:**This determines if your restaurant's name appears in the results when guests are searching for restaurants in a particular neighborhood in a city (i.e., Upper West Side in New York City).
* **Website:** This determines where guests are directed anywhere your website is listed as a hyperlink on Resy channels. *Be sure to include the full link with “http”.*

### Types of Events

Resy supports two types of events:

* **General admission** is best for events where guests can flow freely throughout the event and don't need to stay tied to an assigned space. The amount of tickets you can sell is up to you and is not tied to your floor plan. Examples include open bar fundraisers, New Year's Eve parties, and live music concerts.
* **Assigned seating** is best for events where guests have a pre-determined seat that they will stay at for a majority of the night. You can only sell as many tickets as you have seats on your floor plan. Examples include wine dinners, prix fixe meals, and Valentine's Day reservations.

  

---

  

### What is Remote Waitlist?

* Guests within a short distance of your restaurant will see the option to add themselves to your waitlist. Check out the [Mobile Waitlist City Radius List](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/mobile-waitlist-city-radius-list-B1fGMv7Id) for more details.
* Guests are quoted a dynamic **estimated wait time**based on the last quoted wait time for their party size. Only party sizes 1-4 will be supported.
* Guests will receive all waitlist SMS communications for real-time updates and two-way communication with the host stand.
* Mobile Waitlist is only available on the Resy App and guests can only join **one**Mobile Waitlist at a time.

### Types of Emails

There are three types of emails your guests receive:

* **Confirmation Email:**This is the email guests receive when they make a reservation.
* **Cancellation Reply:**This is the email guests receive confirming that they cancelled their reservation.
* **Change Reply:**This is the email guests receive to confirm any changes they made to their original reservation.

### Tables Attributes

Tables can be set to one or more of the following attributes:

* **Online:** Tables set to “Online” are available for booking by guests through [Resy.com](http://resy.com/), your widget or website, and through the Resy diner app.
* **In-House:** Tables set to “In-House” are available to book in-house through Resy OS. For example, a reservation taken over the phone or in-person.
* **Walk-In:** Tables set to “Walk-in” are **not** available for booking online or in-house and allow you to decide how to use the table in the shift. For example, you have patio tables that are weather dependent, and you do not want them booked ahead of time but can choose to assign day of.
* **Mixed:** Tables that have two or more table attributes assigned throughout the shift are referred to as mixed.
