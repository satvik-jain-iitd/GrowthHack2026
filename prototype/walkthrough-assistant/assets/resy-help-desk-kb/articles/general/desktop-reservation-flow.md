# Booking from Your Dashboard

You can now book reservations directly on the Resy Dashboard, allowing your reservationist to provide a better guest experience and toggle between different venues more efficiently. In addition, the booking process was built to be as flexible as your reservationist, so the steps to book a reservation can be completed in any order they see fit.

**Please note**: This feature is only available for restaurants on our Platform 360 Package. If you're interested in upgrading, please contact your account manager or [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com). 

**In this article**:

* Overview of the Dashboard Booking
* Making your first reservation
* Spread of covers
* Editing a reservation

---

  

## Overview of Dashboard Booking

  

* The "**calendar banner"** at the top of your screen makes it easy to click through different dates, as well as see any day or shift notes that were added by your team
* The "**party size picker"** sits just above the ‘add guest’ panel on the right
* **Time slots** are listed top to bottom on the left
* The "**venue selector"** is in the top right corner to quickly toggle between different restaurants
* Your **spread of covers** is visible in the dashboard booking flow and can be accessed by clicking the up-arrow icon in the bottom left-hand corner of your screen to see what times you can book without overbooking
* **Available reservations** and their table types are in the center of the screen. You can click into each reservation to see details, like if it’s tied to a cancellation policy or pre-payment. If no reservations are available for the date and time selected, there is an option to add the guest to your Notify list or overbook

#### **Do I need to complete these steps in a specific order?**

  

* Absolutely not! We built this tool based on what we heard from our restaurant partners to be as flexible and agile as possible.

## How to book a reservation from the Dashboard

  

1. Log into the Resy Dashboard using your credentials by [clicking here.](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues)
2. Click **"New Reservation"** at the top left.
3. Add your guest's information at any point during the booking flow using the ‘add guest panel’ on the right by typing the guest’s name, phone number, or email address. The guest’s profile will pull up just as if you were using it on the iPad.
	1. If the reservation is being made either through ‘book on behalf,’ like concierge or through an assistant, click ‘add reservation contact’ to include that information.
	2. If you click the three dots on the top-right of the guest profile, you have the option to Google the guest, show or hide notes, merge guest profiles, and update their VIP status.
	3. To add guest notes or tags, click the + to the right of “tags and notes” underneath their name. If you just want to add a note to a specific reservation, like a celebration or allergy, click the + under “visit info.”[[IMAGE: desktop-reservation-flow__img02]]
4. Select the guest’s desired date and party size from the ribbon across the top. Please note dates that have a note, closure or event will be indicated with a red dot.[[IMAGE: desktop-reservation-flow__img03]]
5. Select the shift and reservation time from the panel on the left-hand side. Click into each shift to see times.[[IMAGE: desktop-reservation-flow__img04]]
6. Click into the reservation to view details on any policies, like cancellation fees or pre-payment.
7. Click “review reservation” to confirm the booking.
8. You can apply or remove confirmation emails and add your name if you do not see it already populated.

[[IMAGE: desktop-reservation-flow__img05]]

#### What do the green check marks mean when I make a reservation?

* The green check marks indicate the ideal reservation to book based on the reservations you have on your books, your pacing, and your turn times.

## Spread of Covers

  

You can view your spread of covers for the day in the bottom left-hand corner of the screen. This helps you decide when to overbook. The gray numbers at the top indicate how many covers are booked over how many covers are dictated by your pacing.

* For example, 12/10 means you have 12 covers booked at that time when you have allocated 10 covers to that time in your pacing.

## Editing a Reservation on Dashboard Booking

Steps to edit a booking: 

1. Log into the Resy OS Dashboard [here](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues).
2. Click **"Reports"** from the left-hand menu options.
3. Click **"Reservations"** from the drop-down list.
4. Select the Date of the reservation you wish to edit.
5. Scroll to the reservation you want to edit or cancel and click the three dots to the right (pictured below) and select "Change" to edit the reservation.[[IMAGE: desktop-reservation-flow__img07]]
6. Next, you'll be able to choose a new date, time, or party size.
7. Then confirm the new guest and new reservation before clicking "complete".
