# Loyalist Integration

## Loyalist Integration

The Resy x Loyalist Integration requires a one-time setup to link the platforms. You can initiate this process by reaching out to Loyalist directly with your Resy Venue ID.

### Finding your Resy Venue ID:

1. Log in to your ResyOS Dashboard at: <https://os.resy.com/portal/login>
2. Click on the top-left corner icon – the “Venue” – and the Widget tab.
3. Scroll down the page until you see Venue ID.
4. Record the number to the right of Venue ID.

### Initiating CRM Integration Onboarding with Loyalist:

This section is for Resy venues that are interested in onboarding to Loyalist and do not already have an existing Loyalist account OR if a new venue is opened within an existing venue group Loyalist account.

1. Complete the Venue information in the following email template & send to: [hello@eatloyalist.com](mailto:hello@eatloyalist.com)  
  
*Hi Loyalist,   
  
We would like to onboard our restaurants.   
  
Group Name: [group name]   
Number of Locations: [number of locations]   
Location Names: [location1], [location2]  
  
 Thank you.*
2. Once onboarding has been complete and it is time to set up the connection, log in to eatloyalist.com.
3. Click Settings in the bottom-left corner of the menu bar.[[IMAGE: loyalist-integration__img01]]
4. Select Integrations from the top menu.
5. Scroll to find the Resy tile and select Add Another Venue.[[IMAGE: loyalist-integration__img02]]
6. Enter your Resy VenueID in the space provided and click Connect. You will receive a message letting you know that your Resy integration has been set up successfully.  
  
[[IMAGE: loyalist-integration__img03]]

### Resy Venues with Existing Loyalist Account:

For Resy venues that already have an account with Loyalist, there is no action required from the venue at this time, and the integration will automatically be in place for these venues. If the venue group opens a new venue, they can follow the steps above to initiate the integration for the new venue.

### Loyalist Support Pathways

To request assistance from a team member from Loyalist support or to request offboarding, please contact [support@loyalist.com](mailto:support@loyalist.com). The expected turnaround time for a response will be about ~1 week.
