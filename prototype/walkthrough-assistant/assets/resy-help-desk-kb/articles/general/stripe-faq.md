### Getting Started

To help your restaurant get started using Stripe, we’ve put together [this article](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/getting-started-on-stripe-BkHLWVX9o) with step-by-step instructions on how to set up your Stripe account. 

**Please note:** Prior to filling out any information, your account must be created by someone authorized to sign in on your organization’s behalf. 

### Support

Stripe staff is available 24/7 via chat, email, and phone. They also have a support center that can be accessed [here](https://support.stripe.com/). 

### Payments

To start accepting payments, make sure that you’ve set up your Stripe account. If you haven’t done this, please refer to our Helpdesk Article,[“Getting Started on Stripe.”](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/getting-started-on-stripe-BkHLWVX9o)We recommend checking out the Stripe support document, [“](https://isolate.menlosecurity.com/https://stripe.com/docs/payments/after-the-payment)[After the Paymen](https://stripe.com/docs/payments/after-the-payment)[t](https://stripe.com/docs/payments/after-the-payment)[”](https://isolate.menlosecurity.com/https://stripe.com/docs/payments/after-the-payment) to learn more about what happens after accepting your first payment on Stripe.  

**How do payments appear on the guest’s credit card statement?**

The charge will show up as your restaurant’s name on the guest’s statement.  

**What happens if a payment fails?**

There are a variety of reasons a payment could fail. Each type of failure is handled differently via Stripe and details are provided within the payment’s details in the Dashboard. [Click here](https://stripe.com/docs/declines) to understand more about declines and failed payments.  

**Am I able to send receipts?**

Stripe can automatically send email receipts after a successful payment, or when you refund a payment. Each payment in the Dashboard also has a receipt history where your restaurant can view and send receipts. After locating the transaction, scroll until you see ‘Receipt history’ and click view receipt. For more information on how to customize your receipts, [click here](https://stripe.com/docs/receipts).  

**Please note:** The receipt that Stripe provides **only** shows the total amount of the transaction and is not an itemized receipt.

**What are ‘Payment Links’?**

‘Payment Links’ can be used to accept a payment without building additional standalone websites to sell products such as restaurant merchandise by sharing a reusable link to a checkout page. ‘Payment Links’ can be embedded into email communications, social media channels, or your website.  

[[IMAGE: stripe-faq__img02]]  
  

**Please note:** For additional information about ‘Payment Links’ as well as step-by-step instructions on setting one up for your restaurant, [click here](https://stripe.com/docs/payments/payment-links). 

**How can I track payments that use a ‘Payment Link’?**

You can view all your payments, including ‘Payment Links’ on the Stripe Dashboard under payments.  

**Please note**: 'Payment Links' are **not** connected to a reservation and **not** tracked in Resy OS.  

### Refunds

You can refund charges made to your account, either in whole or in part directly from the Stripe dashboard. Refunds use your available Stripe balance (this does not include any pending balance). If your available balance does **not** cover the amount of the refund, Stripe debits the remaining amount from your bank account.  

**Please note:** Refunds can only be sent back to the original payment method used in a charge. Stripe is unable to send a refund to a different card or bank account.  

**How do I refund a guest in Stripe?**

There are two ways to refund a guest in Stripe. Refunds can be issued on behalf of Stripe, or you can refund the guest using the Stripe dashboard. [Here is an in-depth article](https://stripe.com/docs/refunds) regarding Stripe refunds.  

**Refunds issued on behalf of Stripe:** Stripe submits a refund request to your guest's bank or card issuer. Your guest sees the refund as a credit 5-10 business days later, depending on the bank. In addition, Stripe will also send an email to your guest notifying them of the refund.  

**Refunding a payment using the Stripe Dashboard:**

1. Find the payment to be refunded in the [payments overview](https://dashboard.stripe.com/payments) page.
2. Click the ••• icon to the right of the charge. From the resulting menu, select ‘Refund payment’.
3. By default, you will issue a full refund. For a partial refund, enter a different amount to be refunded.
4. Select a reason for the refund. If you select ‘Other,’ you must provide an explanatory note that is attached to the refund.
5. Click ‘Refund’.

**Please note**: The Dashboard supports bulk refunding of full payments, but you cannot bulk refund partial payments.  

**How long does it take for a guest to receive their refund?**

Your guest should see their refund as a credit 5-10 business days later, depending upon the bank.  

**Where can I view my refunds?**

You can view refunds under the 'Payments' section in your Stripe Dashboard. 

[[IMAGE: stripe-faq__img03]]  
  

**Can I cancel a refund?**

Refunds are processed immediately and when issued they cannot be canceled. In addition, you can issue more than one refund against a charge, but you cannot refund a total greater than the original charge amount.  

**What if a refund fails?**

A refund can fail if the guest’s bank or card issuer has been unable to process it correctly (for example, a closed bank account or a problem with the card). If a refund fails, the bank returns the refunded amount to Stripe, and it is added back to your Stripe account balance. The process can take up to 30 days (about 4 and a half weeks) from the post date.  

### Payouts

Stripe makes payouts to your bank account. You can view a list of all your payouts and the date that you expect to receive them in your bank account in the Stripe Dashboard.  

**Please note**: The first payout will be available seven days after you have added banking information and successfully taken your first payment on Stripe.  

[[IMAGE: stripe-faq__img04]]  
  

**When will I receive my payouts?**

The default payout schedule is automatic and daily with a three-business day [payout speed](https://stripe.com/docs/payouts#payout-speed), where the funds paid out daily would be from three business days preceding. You can change this in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com/account/payouts) to weekly or monthly. When selecting a weekly or monthly schedule, you can specify the day of the week or month that you want payouts to arrive in your bank account.  

**What happens if a payout fails?**

If your bank cannot receive payment for any reason, your bank will send the funds back to Stripe. If a payment has failed, you will be notified via email and on the Stripe Dashboard. Click here for potential reasons a payout might fail. It is important to make sure that the bank account information you provided is correct, because Stripe will send funds using that information.  

**Payout reporting options:**

1. If you are on automatic payouts, you can export a report of payouts from your [Payouts dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com/payouts).  

[[IMAGE: stripe-faq__img05]]  

2. For detailed reporting and reconciliation, we recommend using the [Balance Report](https://dashboard.stripe.com/balance). This report details all transactions that factor into your running Stripe account balance such as charges, refunds, and disputes.

### Reporting

**How do I access my Stripe reporting?**

Go to the reporting tab on your Stripe dashboard to view your reports. 

[[IMAGE: stripe-faq__img06]]  

**What are the most frequented reports?**

1. **Activity Report:** This report shows all charges, including refunds, that enter your Stripe account and is listed out by the date of charge.
2. **Balance Report:**This report is a summary of your Stripe balance activity.
3. **Payout Reconciliation Report:** This is a disbursement report.

### Ownership Transfer

The current owner of a Stripe account can transfer ownership to someone else in various ways, like transferring to an existing Administrator or a new individual. If the current owner is no longer with the company or the Owner Administrator account is inaccessible, contact Stripe Support for assistance and provide the necessary documentation and authorization. Stripe support will need the following to verify that the requester should be given account ownership:  
   
 A letter from a company signatory, which confirms and details the change of Company Representative and the transfer of ownership of the Stripe account. The letter should be on your company's letterhead and authorize the appointment of the new Company Representative, as well as the transfer of ownership to a specified user.Please include the following information:

* Business name
* Business tax ID
* Business address
* Previous Company
* Representative’s name and date of birth (if available)
* New Company
* Representative’s name and date of birth
* New Owner Administrator’s email address (this should be the one you use to log into Stripe)

Please review this [Stripe Help Desk](https://support.stripe.com/questions/change-the-owner-of-a-stripe-account) page for full details on ownership transfers.
