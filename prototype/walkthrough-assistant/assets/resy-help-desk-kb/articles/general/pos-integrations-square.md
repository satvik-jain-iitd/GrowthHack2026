# POS Integrations: Square

## POS Integrations: Square

### In this article:

* **Integrating Square with Resy**

**Please note:** Restaurants must be on the Square for Restaurants Plus package to implement the Square x Resy Integration. This integration is only available to US restaurants.

Additionally, for the integration to function correctly, the restaurant must have [floorplans set up](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6427-building-your-floor-plan) within Square and use Square’s table functionality.

### Integrating Square with Resy

**Enable the Integration in Square** 

1. Click [this link](https://partnergateway.americanexpress.com/marketing/v1/rooam/si/square/auth/1/oauth) and sign in to your Square Dashboard.
2. Select Settings > App integrations then select Visit App Marketplace, or go directly to the [Square App Marketplace](https://squareup.com/us/en/app-marketplace).
3. Browse apps by category or search for a third-party integration.
4. To connect the app with your Square account, select Get Started.
5. Follow the prompts to authorize.

**After completing the steps in Square**, please use [this link](https://resy.chilipiper.com/round-robin/resy-pos-integration-cloud) to set up a time with our team to enable the integration.

After completing the steps above, you will be able to leverage the [POS features](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/pos-integrations-HkUQN3s5u) within Resy powered by the integration.
