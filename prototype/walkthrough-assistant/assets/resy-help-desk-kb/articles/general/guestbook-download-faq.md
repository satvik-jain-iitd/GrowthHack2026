# Guestbook Download FAQ

You can now access and download your guestbook directly from the ResyOS Dashboard. With this feature, you can deliver a better guest experience and reach out through various communication channels for marketing purposes.

In this article:

* Setting Up User Permissions
* Downloading your ResyOS Guestbook
* ResyOS Guestbook Filters

---

 

### Setting Up User Permissions

You can access your restaurant’s complete guestbook with the proper user permissions. If your ResyOS account already has the permission to manage other users, you will automatically have access to the guestbook and can control and create user permissions for others. Please ensure the correct users have the **"****Manage****guestbook"** option toggled on.

**Please note:** For full capability, please ensure the correct users have "**Manage****guestbook**" toggled on. 

[[IMAGE: guestbook-download-faq__img01]]

**Want more information?  Learn how to add, edit, remove and manage all user permission and logins in**[**this article.**](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/user-permissions-r1zCWDmU_)

### Downloading your Resy OS Guestbook

**Please note:** Only users with permission to "**Manage****guestbook**" will have access to this section of the Resy Dashboard.

1. Log in to the [Resy Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/venues) using your credentials.

2. Click **"Guests"** from the left-hand menu options, then choose **"****Guestbook"**.

| ***Please Note****: When viewing your full Guestbook, you'll see an icon indicating each guests Marketing Preference. The image below is a breakdown of what the icons mean.**[[IMAGE: guestbook-download-faq__img02]]* |
| --- |

#### ResyOS Guestbook Filters

3. Setting up filters: 

[[IMAGE: guestbook-download-faq__img03]]

 **· Service Period Date :** This filter defaults to ‘is any time’ which is your all-time guestbook. You can set your date filter to a specific date range, last week, last month or last year.

        i. Filter by service date to target guests who have dined with you recently to reach out about a new menu or guests who dined with you   last holiday season to encourage them to book early.

**· Visit Count :** The default is set to greater than or equal to 1 which encompasses your full guestbook. Increase this number to sort by guests who have dined with you more often.

 **· Opted-in to Restaurant Marketing*:**When guests book with you through Resy, they opt-in or out of marketing. You may only send email marketing communications to guests who have opted in. When downloading your guestbook, ensure you have selected Is Marketable = Yes. You and your affiliated restaurant(s) are solely responsible for any interactions with customers and other third parties.

 **· Tags:** Type in guest tags from Resy OS to sort by Wine Lovers, Friends of Chef, Vegetarians, etc. 

        ii. Filter guests by tags or VIP status to pull lists for specific events or marketing campaigns. Invite Wine Lovers to your next tasting event, or target VIPs to encourage them to purchase gift cards. 

 **· VIP:** Select "Yes" to pull a list of just your VIPs and "No" to pull the rest of your guestbook. To download your complete guestbook, choose "is any value". 

 

4. To download your newly filtered guestbook, hover over the globe icon within the report and click on the action's menu (   ) to the left of the globe.

[[IMAGE: guestbook-download-faq__img04]]

6. Then, select "**Download data**".

[[IMAGE: guestbook-download-faq__img05]]

7. Click "**Advanced data options**" to open the menu and under "***Number of rows to include****"*, select "**All results**" > Click "**Download**".

[[IMAGE: guestbook-download-faq__img06]]

| **Please note:** The guestbook file may take a few minutes to generate and download to your computer. |
