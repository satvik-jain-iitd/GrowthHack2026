# Open a Shift on a Day you are Normally Closed

Open and adjust your shift when there is a special holiday or occasion. 

**In this article:**

* [Open a Shift on a Day you are Normally Closed](#part1)

---

 

## Open a Shift on a Day you are Normally Closed

  

If your shift for this special day will have the same settings and hours as your normal shifts, copy your normal base shifts to that day by:

1. Log in to the [ResyOS Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal).
2. From the left-hand menu, select “Service”, followed by “Shift Settings”.

[[IMAGE: opening-a-shift-on-a-day-you-are-normally-closed__img01]]

  

3. Go to the shift you'd like to copy and click on the three horizontal dots.

4. Click “Copy Shift”.

  

[[IMAGE: opening-a-shift-on-a-day-you-are-normally-closed__img02]]

  

5. Under Step 1, select the day of the week this special day occurs on. The “Start Date” should be the date of this special day and the “End Date” should be the following day so it does not repeat. 

[[IMAGE: opening-a-shift-on-a-day-you-are-normally-closed__img03]]6. Click “Copy Shift” on the bottom-right corner to save.
