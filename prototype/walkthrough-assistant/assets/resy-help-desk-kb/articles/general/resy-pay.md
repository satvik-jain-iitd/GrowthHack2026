### **What is Resy Pay?[[IMAGE: resy-pay__img01]]**

Resy Pay is a mobile payment platform that replaces the traditional consumer and restaurant steps of dropping a check and paying with a physical card for in-person dining. Resy Pay also enables eligible American Express® Card Members to use their Membership Rewards points towards a meal in restaurants.

### **How does Resy Pay Work?**

Resy Pay provides restaurants with two mobile payment options to offer their guests. The first option is a QR code on the check or a QR code printed on a table tent. Both options allow the guest to use their mobile device to scan the QR code and begin the payment flow and view, split, and pay for their check.

### 

### **How does Resy Pay benefit our restaurant partners?**

**Maximizes revenue**

Resy Pay reduces turn times to maximize covers so you can serve more guests with fewer staff. Resy Pay comes with a transaction fee of **2.9% + 30 cents, a**potential increase in tips for staff, and the ability for American Express Card Members to pay with points.

 

**Easy to implement**

Resy Pay integrates with most POS Systems in the U.S. and installs in as little as 10 minutes.* Quick support is always available, with an average 60 second response time and 2-minute resolution time.

 

**Improved guest experience**

Guests don’t have to wait for the check to arrive and can pay as soon as they are ready via QR code. They can easily split the check with friends, and, for an even faster checkout, use Apple Pay, Google Pay, or a card saved on their Resy account.
