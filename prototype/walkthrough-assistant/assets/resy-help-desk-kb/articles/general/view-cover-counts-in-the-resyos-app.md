# View Cover Counts on the Calendar in ResyOS

You have the ability to view your covers by shift and total covers for the day on the calendar in the ResyOS App. This is an easy way to see a breakdown of your cover counts for a particular day. 

**In this article:**

* How to View Cover Counts on the Calendar in ResyOS

---

  

## How to View Cover Counts on the Calendar in ResyOS

In the ResyOS app, 

1. Tap the Calendar icon on the upper right.
2. Tap the 'person in a circle' icon.

From here, you will see letters and numbers on each day in the Calendar.  

* B = Breakfast covers.
* L = Lunch covers.
* D = Dinner covers.
* A = Total covers for that day.
