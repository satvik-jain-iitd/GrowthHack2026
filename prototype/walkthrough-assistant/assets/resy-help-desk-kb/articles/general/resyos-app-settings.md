## Device Settings

### **Reservations**

* **Auto-seat by dragging from the reservation list to table:** This gives you the ability to drag a reservation from your booked list to a table on your floor plan.
* **Display VIP name of reservation list:** If the guest has a VIP tag, the tag will be displayed by the guest’s name on the reservation list.
* **Show Visit Notes on reservation list:** If the guest has any Visit Notes for their reservation, you will see it displayed next to the guest’s name on the reservation list.
* **Show Resy-assigned tables on reservation list and floor plan**: Toggle this setting on if you want the ability to see auto-assigned reservations on the floor plan view and your reservation list. If this is off, you will not see the guests whose reservations were auto-assigned on the reservation list or floor plan; you will only see them in grid view.
* **Prompt table assignment after booking unassigned reservation:** When booking an unassigned reservation, you will be taken directly to the floor plan view to assign a table to the reservation.
* **Auto-select default credit card:** Anytime you book a reservation that requires payment information, the guest’s default credit card on file will automatically be selected for the transaction.
* **Alert when reservations overlap:** If you are moving around or reassigning reservations to different tables, this setting will notify you if the reservation’s turn time overlaps with another reservation that is assigned to the table you are moving it to.
* **Display all guest and visit note details on guest profile:** All guest and visit notes created for the guest for your venue will be displayed on their profile.
* **Guest Name Display:** You have the option to display guest names by first and last name or last and first name.

### **Waitlist**

* **Sort By Time Added:** Sorts your guest on your waitlist by the order of the time they were added to the waitlist.
* **Show reservation time as Time Added + Quoted Waited Time:** Shows your guests on the waitlist reservation time as the quoted wait time combined with the time you added the guest to the waitlist. For example, a guest was added to the waitlist at 4:30 pm with a quoted wait time of 30 minutes, so their reservation time will show as 5:00 pm if this setting is turned on. Otherwise, if the setting is off the guest on the waitlist will only display the time they were added to the waitlist.
* **Combine waitlist and reservations:** This setting is only available if you have “Show reservation time as Time Added + Quoted Waited Time” turned on. This setting combines your waitlist guests and your guests with reservations in one list instead of them being listed in separate sections.
* **Time Interval:** You have the option to offer quoted wait times in intervals of 5 or 15 minutes.

For more information on the Waitlist and how it works, [click here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-manage-your-waitlist:-adding-messaging-and-seating-guests-BJHGGvmId) to view an article explaining how to manage your Waitlist.

### **Quick Replies**

Here you can create canned messages that you can manually send guests from their profile in Resy OS. [Click here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/text-message-quick-replies-SywMGDm8d) for more information on how to create, edit, and send Quick Replies.

### **Printing**

Here is where you will connect your chit printer to the Resy OS app. [Click here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/resyos-recommended-hardware-and-accessories-SyvVbP78_) to view a list of compatible chit printers. If you are having trouble connecting your chit printer, [click here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/troubleshooting-for-bluetooth-chit-printers-rycyGwXId) to view an article containing troubleshooting steps for connecting your chit printers.

### **Floor Plan**

**Show duration of seated reservations:** This setting allows you to see the amount of time a reservation has been seated on the floor plan view.

### **About**

Here you can see:

* App Version
* iOS Version
* Device model
* Available space
* The User you are logged in as
* Clear cache

***Please note****: You’ll want to have this information pulled up if you are contacting Resy Support about an issue with Resy OS. This information is often used when troubleshooting.*

## Group Settings

### **Tags**

Here is where you will manage your guest and visit tags. [Click here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-add-guest-and-visit-tags-to-a-reservation-in-resy-os-r14ZzPXL_) for more information on how to manage your guest and visit tags. 

***Please note****: If you delete a tag, you will not be able to add it back in Resy OS. You will need to*[*contact Resy Support*](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-contact-resy-support-Bk_JGP7Lu)*in order to have it added back.*
