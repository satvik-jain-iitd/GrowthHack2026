# Export Stripe Reports

You can pull reports in Stripe for transactions processed through Resy. Below, we'll list the steps for the different types of reports in Stripe. 

**In this article**:

* Transactions Report
* Event Revenue Report
* Disbursement Report

| *You will need to be in the **Stripe Dashboard** in order to pull these reports. To access your Stripe Dashboard, i**n the**[ResyOS Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/)**> Select "**Venue**" > Click the "**Transactions**"tab > Click "**Stripe Dashboard**".* |
| --- |

---

## 

## Transactions Report

1. On the "Payments" page, click "**Export**" on the right side of the page.
2. For "Date Range", select on of the existing option or select "**Custom**" and update the dates to reflect the date(s) desired.
3. Click "**Export**".

You can use filters if you are wanting to report on specific transactions. 

**Commonly Used Reporting Filters:**

* Date = Specific date/date range.
* Amount = Specific transaction amount/amount range.
* Status = Specific payment statuses.

## Event Revenue Report

1. On the "Payments" page, click "**Export**" on the right side of the page.
2. For "Date Range", select "**Custom**" and update the dates to reflect the date the event tickets went live through (including) the date of the event.
3. Click "**Export**".
4. Then, sort the results based on the reservation date or reservation name to collect the data for your event.

---

  

## Disbursement Report

1. Go to the "Reports" tab and select the "**Payout reconciliation**" report.
2. Click the dates to enter the disbursement date.
3. Go to the "Payout Reconciliation" section and click "**Download**".
4. For the "Report Format" select "**Itemized**".
5. For the "Reporting Category" select "**All**".
6. For "Columns" click "**Show**".
7. Scroll down to "Payment Meta Data" and select tax, gratuity, service fee, reservation date, etc.
8. Click "**Download**".

  

| Reporting Fields to Note | |
| --- | --- |
| item_description | Add-ons the guest selected at the time of booking. |
| account_name | Guest name. |
| product_type | Reservation or Cancellation. |
| sub_total | Pre-tax cost of the reservation. |

  

*For more information on the different reports you can pull in Stripe, click [here](https://stripe.com/docs/reports) to visit Stripe's Knowledge-base.*
