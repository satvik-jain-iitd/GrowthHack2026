# Concierge Registration

Hotel/concierge's can submit an application to be registered as a Concierge. Guest profiles that have concierge permissions have the ability to book on behalf of their guests. Concierges can book multiple reservations in one evening for multiple guests.

  

In this article:

* Concierge Application
* Concierge Profile Features

---

  

### Concierge Application

Guests can apply to become registered as a concierge directly from their [Resy.com](//Resy.com) profile. 

1. Sign in to your account on [Resy.com](//Resy.com).
2. Click the profile picture on the upper right. [[IMAGE: concierge-registration__img01]]
3. On the **Profile** tab, go down to the **Concierge Registration** section.
4. Complete the fields and click **Save**.[[IMAGE: concierge-registration__img02]]

If approved, the guest will get an email letting them know their account has been approved for concierge access. 

---

  

### Concierge Profile Features

**Book on Behalf of:**Guests with concierge access can book on behalf of others when making reservations online through [Resy.com](http://Resy.com). They'll see the option in the booking flow to **'book for me' or 'book on behalf of another guest'.** Booking on behalf of a guest will allow the concierge to enter the guest's name, email, and phone number to be listed as the primary reservation contact in Resy OS.

[[IMAGE: concierge-registration__img03]]  

  
**Concierge Reservations View:**Guests with concierge access will see a new **Concierge Reservations** view in their profile menu on [Resy.com.](%5C%22http://Resy.com%5C%22) This will separate their past and upcoming Book on Behalf of reservations from those booked on their personal account.

[[IMAGE: concierge-registration__img04]]

  
**Multiple Reservations Permission:**As a registered concierge, they are allowed to book multiple reservations per service period online. This is a restriction for standard Resy guests to eliminate fake reservations and reduce no-shows.

View our [Concierge FAQs](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/concierge-faqs-ByCAvm8O) for more information about concierge access.
