# Stripe Reporting

### **How do I access reports in Stripe?**

You can access reports directly from your Stripe Dashboard by clicking on the “Reports” tab in the menu bar. All reports will be listed on the left-hand side under “Overview”.

[[IMAGE: stripe-reporting__img01]]

  

### **Popular Reports**

These frequented reports are the most relevant to daily operations: 

* **Balance Report:** Reconcile your Stripe balance and download your categorized transaction history.
* **Payout reconciliation:** View transactions that have settled into your bank account including a categorized breakdown for each payment.
* **Revenue recognition**: View key information like revenue and deferred revenue. These are automatically generated reports that you can view in the Stripe Dashboard and export as CSV files.

[[IMAGE: stripe-reporting__img02]] 

**Please note:** [Click here](https://stripe.com/docs/reports)for more information on additional reporting capabilities provided by Stripe that could be useful to your restaurant.

**How do I filter my reports by date and time?**

To customize the date and time for your reports: 

1. Select the report you would like to adjust.
2. Click the “Custom” button.
3. Select your desired date range and time zone.

**Please note:**You can automatically schedule this report by selecting “Schedule”. 

**How do I download my reports?**

To export your report, click the “Download” button found at the top right of the report. A pop-up screen will appear where you can customize what fields will be displayed in your downloaded report.
