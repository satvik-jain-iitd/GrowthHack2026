# POS Integration: Digital Chits Feature

*This feature is currently available only for partners using Toast POS*

Restaurant partners can now unlock the Digital Chits feature by integrating ResyOS and their Toast POS system. This is available for restaurants using Toast POS where the integration has been configured, and the feature is enabled. 

Digital Chits helps restaurant staff personalize service by displaying relevant Resy guest and reservation details directly inside Toast devices during service. Instead of switching between systems or relying on printed notes, front-of-house teams can view important dining context where they are already taking orders and managing the guest experience.

---

## **Features of Digital Chits**

* Displays Resy guest and reservation context inside Toast POS terminals and handhelds. The data that is sent between ResyOS and Toast POS may include:
	
		1. Occasion
		2. VIP Tags
		3. Guest Tags
		4. Visit Tag
		5. Global Dining Access (GDA)
		6. Allergies
		7. Special Requests
		8. Guest Notes
		9. Visit Notes
* Helps front-of-house staff reference relevant guest information during service.
* Supports a more personalized dining experience by making reservation data available where staff are already working.
* Digital chit data remains available only in a limited day-of-service context.

## **Enabling Digital Chits**

Digital Chits is available once the Resy integration has been activated within the Toast marketplace. Please click [here](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/pos-integrations-toast-Hyvj9cpKZx) for the steps to enable Toast POS integration for your restaurant. Once active, the Digital Chits feature is accessible within the ResyOS Dashboard (from the left-hand menu panel, select Venue > POS Settings). OS users must have the “manage integrations” permissions enabled within User Permissions to access this page.

[[IMAGE: pos-integration-digital-chits-feature__img02]]  

To learn more about enabling Digital Chits for your restaurant, contact your Resy account team or support representative.  
  

### **Digital Chit Checklist**

Ensure the following requirements are met for Digital Chits to appear in Toast:

* The Toast Digital Chit feature must be toggled on in your venue’s ResyOS POS Settings page.
* Table names match exactly between Resy and Toast.
	1. Example: B1 = B1
	2. B 1, b1, 1, or any other variation will not match.
* Seat the reservation in Resy first before opening orders in Toast
* Guest first name and phone number are entered on the reservation.
* Reservation notes and tags are added before seating the reservation in Resy.

Missing any of the above steps will result in the digital chit not showing up in Toast properly.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

**What is Digital Chits?**

Digital Chits displays key Resy guest and reservation information inside Toast POS terminals and handheld devices. A Digital Chit is intended to appear on Toast devices, so staff can reference relevant guest details during service. This helps hosts, servers, and managers stay informed and deliver more personalized hospitality from the first interaction through the dining experience.

**Who is eligible for Digital Chits?**

Digital Chits is available for restaurants that use:

* ResyOS
* Toast POS (terminal or handheld devices)
* An enabled and configured Resy + Toast POS integration

**How does Digital Chits work?**

When a reservation is marked as seated in ResyOS and is connected to a valid Toast table, Digital Chits surfaces guest and reservation context in Toast. This allows staff using Toast POS terminals or handhelds to view relevant information while serving the table.

**When does the Digital Chit appear?**

When a reservation is seated in ResyOS and matched to a Toast table, that table will appear purple on the Toast Table Service screen. For chits to attach to the proper table, the reservation must be marked as seated or partially seated **before** the check is opened in Toast.

#### **How can I opt in to Digital Chits, or make updates to my Chits integration status?**

If you would like to opt in or make changes to your restaurant’s Digital Chits integration status, you can contact our Support Team through:

* Chat within the ResyOS app or Dashboard
* Email at [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com)

[[IMAGE: pos-integration-digital-chits-feature__img03]]

Table with a Digital Chit attached will see table highlighted in grey with purple bolder.

When a staff member selects the purple table, the Digital Chit will open automatically for review before an order can be placed. After reviewing the chit, staff can close it and continue placing the order as usual.

Once an order has been started and items have been added, the table will no longer appear purple, and the Digital Chit will no longer open automatically when the table is selected.

To view the Digital Chit again, staff can use the re-entry button in Toast:

1. Go to the table’s order screen from Table Service.
2. Select the re-entry button labeled with the guest’s name on the POS terminal or party details on the Toast handheld.

**Can staff access Digital Chits after service?**

No, Digital Chits are intended to support active service workflows and are not retained after service. After an order is paid and closed in Toast, the Digital Chit is no longer displayed on the POS.  Future and historic bookings are not loaded or retained, and Toast POS does not maintain a separate historical Digital Chits view.
