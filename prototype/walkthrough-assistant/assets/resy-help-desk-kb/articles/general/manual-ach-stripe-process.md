# Manual ACH Stripe Process

## Manual ACH Stripe Process

When putting payment on file to pay for your Resy subscription, you have the option to pay via ACH (Automated Clearing House) or credit card. 

If you elect to do ACH and opt to manually add your account (instead of automatically verifying through your bank), Stripe will make a small deposit to your bank account which you will have to verify to confirm your ownership of the account. This process takes one to two business days.

**Please note:** Newly onboarded accounts cannot move forward with next steps until the verification of a manual ACH is completed. We encourage you to use a credit card or automated ACH login if you have a short timeline for joining Resy; you can always switch payment methods later.

 Here’s what to expect:

* If you select 'card' payment method, you will be presented with the following screen:

 

[[IMAGE: manual-ach-stripe-process__img01]]

* If you select 'Bank Account', you will be presented with the following screen. You can search for your bank account and automatically log in if your bank supports it.

[[IMAGE: manual-ach-stripe-process__img02]]

[[IMAGE: manual-ach-stripe-process__img03]]

* If your bank does not show up after you search for it, you can enter your bank details manually instead. As a reminder, verifying your account this way **will take****one to two business days.**
	+ When you click the option to enter banking details manually, it will ask for your routing number and account number. Once this is submitted, you will receive a micro deposit of $0.01 one to two days later with a 6-digit verification code in the transaction description. Next, you will receive an email where you are required to verify that code to ensure that you have ownership over the account prior to Resy onboarding.

 

[[IMAGE: manual-ach-stripe-process__img04]]

[[IMAGE: manual-ach-stripe-process__img05]]

**Please note:** We cannot finalize your Resy contract until this verification process has been completed. For example, if you complete this on a Friday, it may not be until Monday or Tuesday that you receive the deposit and can then verify the account, delaying the closing of your Resy contract. 

If you decide you'd like to add a different payment method, you can do so from within the portal by following the steps below:

1. Clicking the three bars in the upper left-hand corner.
2. Go to “Payment Methods”.
3. Under “Manage Payment Methods”, select “+ Add New Payment Method”.
