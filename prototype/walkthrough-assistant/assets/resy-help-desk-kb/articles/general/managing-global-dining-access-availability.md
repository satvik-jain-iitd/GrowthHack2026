# Managing Global Dining Access Availability

#### The Global Dining Access (GDA) inventory allows you to allocate online tables to eligible American Express® Premium Card Members. The Global Dining Access Exclusive Booking Channel can be applied to your tables in the availability section of your Shift Settings.

Please note that as a member of the GDA Program, you are obligated to have your contracted tables and times allocated to GDA in your books.

## Add Global Dining Access Exclusive Groups to Your Shifts

Use the Service Settings tab in the Resy OS Dashboard to assign your channel to a recurring shift or as a single day shift. 

**Applying channels to your recurring shifts**:

1. Click on “Service”.
2. Click “Shift Settings”.
3. Click the three horizontal dots on the shift and select “Edit Shift”.
4. From “Step 3 Availability” in the shift editor, click “More Customization”. [[IMAGE: managing-global-dining-access-availability__img02]]
5. Select the tab labeled “Exclusive Booking Channels” on the top right. This section will display the table attributes you have set up as the base availability for reference (i.e., Online, In-House, or Walk-In). [[IMAGE: managing-global-dining-access-availability__img03]]
6. Click a table number or select specific times on a table throughout the shift and choose the Global Dining Access channel on the left side panel. From the side panel, you can also edit the base availability to online or in-house if you choose.
	* **Please note**: When a channel expires, the tables will always default to the base availability applied to that shift. [[IMAGE: managing-global-dining-access-availability__img04]]
7. Click “Update Availability” in the bottom-right corner and “Update Shift” to save.

**Applying channels to a single day:**

1. Click “Service”.
2. Select “Calendar”.
3. Click on the date you'd like to edit.
4. Select “Single Day Edit” and choose desired shift (i.e., Lunch, Dinner).
5. Proceed to Step 4 in the “**Applying channels to your recurring shifts**” section above.

The Global Dining Access “A” icon will display in the timeline view on the Resy OS App to indicate the table has a GDA channel applied.
