# Transactions Settings (US)

If you are hosting any prepaid inventory on Resy, you'll need to enter in your banking information and set up your Sales Tax Rates on the “Transaction Settings” page.  

**In this article:**

* [Submit your Banking Information](#part1)
* [Setting Up Tax Rates](#part2)
	+ Add, edit, or delete Tax Types
* [Prepaid Reservations and Events](#part3)
* [Add-on Items](#part4)
* [User Permissions](#part5)

## 

## **Submit your Banking Information**

  

The bank account in your Resy Dashboard settings allows for the direct deposit of funds collected through Resy. This includes prepaid reservations, events, cancellation fees, and items. 

**Please note:** Bank information can be set up and adjusted in the Stripe Dashboard. Your account information is stored securely with our third-party processor, Stripe. If you need to change your account, please log in to the Stripe Dashboard for your restaurant.

1. In the [Resy OS Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/), click "Venue" from the left-hand menu.
2. From the dropdown menu, click “Transaction Settings”.
3. Click “Stripe” on the left-hand panel.  
  
  
[[IMAGE: managing-transactions-settings-for-us-partners__img01]]
4. If you don't have your banking information on file, you can log in to the Stripe Dashboard to set this up.

## **Setting Up Tax Rates**

  

Once your banking information is saved, you will be able to set up your tax rates. 

1. In the [Resy OS Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/), click "Venue" from the left-hand menu.
2. From the dropdown menu, click “Transaction Settings”.
3. Click on “Tax Type” in the left-hand panel. There is one required tax rate which is for prepaid reservations. There is also an option to specify tax rates based on the category of an item.
4. Tap on “Set Up Sales Tax”.

  
**Adding a Tax Type**

If you're selling an item that does not fall under the Resy default categories, you can add a new tax type by:

1. Clicking on the blue “Add Tax Type” button on the right-hand corner of the “Tax Type” window.
2. In the pop-up window, type in the “Tax Type Name”, “Tax Rate”, and “Change Log Note” that explains who made the request, when the request was made, and the channel it was made from (email, ustomer, etc.)

#### [[IMAGE: managing-transactions-settings-for-us-partners__img02]]

#### 

**Editing a Tax Type**

If you need to edit an existing tax type, tap on the three horizontal dots in the top-right corner of the tax rate field to open the option to edit, delete, or view the change log of a tax type. 

The change log is a record of any changes made to the tax rate in the Resy Dashboard. 

**Deleting a Tax Type**

You can delete any tax types you added into the system.

**Please note:** You'll not be able to delete any active tax types. Resy default tax types cannot be deleted.

## **Prepaid Reservations and Events**

* When creating a prepaid reservation, you will need to set the base price. You'll have the option to add paid policies as well. For gratuity, there will be a default “no tip” option and you'll have the ability to add three additional values of your choosing as well.

[[IMAGE: managing-transactions-settings-for-us-partners__img03]]  

  

* When creating prepaid reservations and events or selling add-on items, there will be a guest price preview underneath the base price field. This displays the purchase price of your prepaid inventory.

**Please note:**If you have not set up an account in “Banking Info”, you'll be prompted to do so before creating any prepaid reservations. Likewise, if you have not set a tax type for your prepaid reservations or events, you'll be unable to use that tax type until a tax rate is applied.

[[IMAGE: managing-transactions-settings-for-us-partners__img04]]

 

## **Add-on Items**

Similar to your prepaid inventory, you can choose the tax type for any add-on items. You'll have the option to view the purchase price of an add-on by tapping on guest price preview. If you don't have a tax type set for an item, you'll be able to create one during the create item workflow. 

[[IMAGE: managing-transactions-settings-for-us-partners__img05]]

  

  

  

## **User Permissions**

You'll have the ability to manage who on your team can set up and edit taxes in the “Users” section. “Set and update tax rates” can be toggled on to give operators the ability to edit taxes. If it toggles off, users will be able to view the tax rate but won't be able to add or edit tax rates.

[[IMAGE: managing-transactions-settings-for-us-partners__img06]]
