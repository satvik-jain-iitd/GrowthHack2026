# Transaction Settings (CAN)

If you’re hosting any prepaid inventory on Resy, you’ll need to enter in your banking information on the “Transaction Settings” tab.

**In this article:**

* Submit your Banking Information
* Prepaid Reservations and Events
* Add-on Items
* User Permissions

## **Submit your Banking Information**

The bank account in your Resy Dashboard settings allows for the direct deposit of funds collected through Resy. This includes prepaid reservations, events, cancellation fees, and items.  

**Please note:** Bank information can be set up and adjusted in the Stripe Dashboard. Your account information is stored securely with our third-party processor, Stripe. If you need to change your account, please log in to the Stripe Dashboard for your restaurant.

1. In the [Resy OS Dashboard](https://os.resy.com/portal/), click the graph icon next to the restaurant name.
2. From the dropdown menu, click “Venue”.
3. Select “Transaction Settings” from the “Venue Option” tab.
4. Click “Stripe Dashboard".  
  
[[IMAGE: transaction-settings-can__img01]]
5. If you don't have your banking information on file, you can log in to the Stripe Dashboard to set this up.

              [[IMAGE: transaction-settings-can__img02]]

  

## **Prepaid Reservations and Events**

When creating a prepaid reservation, you will need to set the base price. You will have the option to add paid policies as well.  

**Please note:***Resy OS does not allow**Quebec**venues to present gratuity options to users at checkout. This is necessary to ensure compliance with laws in **Quebec**that regulate commercial tipping practices. See example of the dashboard settings below.*  
 

[[IMAGE: transaction-settings-can__img03]]

  

  

***Please Note****: Our Restaurant Partners located in CAN will not be able to set up tax rates within Resy. The base prices of inventory a restaurant sells must be tax inclusive. Resy will not display a tax amount or rate on the checkout screen to the guest booking nor the receipt that is emailed to the guest.*

*Consumers will be alerted to the tax-inclusive pricing during the booking process:**[[IMAGE: transaction-settings-can__img04]]*

***Please Note****: Resy is not the official provider of an invoice for any inventory sold on behalf of the venue. If a customer requests an official invoice with itemized tax line item, or has questions regarding tax amount or rate, they must contact the restaurant to receive it.**This includes any applicable taxes per your jurisdiction including VAT, Goods and Service Tax, Provincial Tax or other.*
