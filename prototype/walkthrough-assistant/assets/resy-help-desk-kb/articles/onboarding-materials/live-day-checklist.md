# Going Live on Resy

This article has a list of items to review as you go live on Resy.[Click here for printable PDF](https://www.dropbox.com/s/7qyu7xsy2rjukil/Resy_Live%20Day%20Checklist.pdf?dl=0).

#### **In this article:**

* View your listing on [Resy.com](//Resy.com) and confirm listed items
* View the Widget on your website
* Review your Shifts and Table Inventory
* Review your Floor Plan in ResyOS
* Review date closures including holidays and scheduled closed dates

  

---

  

### View your listing on Resy.com and confirm the following:

* Reservation availability
* Photos
* Venue & Reservation Copy
* Address & Map
* Social Media Links

  

---

## 

### View the widget on your website and confirm the following:

* Widget is installed correctly
* Guests are able to book

### Review your shifts and table inventory

**You can manage all of your shift details through our**[**Online Dashboard**](http://os.resy.com/portal). Once logged in:

1. Click "**Service"** from the left-hand menu options.
2. Click "**Shift Settings"***from the drop-down menu* to make changes to your base shifts including pacing, turn times, and table availability.
3. Click "**Calendar"** from the drop-down menu to make single day edits and close dates.

  

**You can also view your inventory in the ResyOS Timeline View:**

* **Online:**Indicated by the dark shaded squares in the timeline view. Can be booked through consumer channels. Consumer booking options are also dependent on pacing
* **In-House:** Indicated by a house icon in the timeline view. Reserved for in-house bookings. Can only be booked through Resy OS and the Dashboard
* **Walk-In:** Indicated by the walking icon in the timeline view. Reserved for walk-ins only. Cannot be booked through any channel including Resy OS, Resy App, or Resy.com
* **Pacing:** Indicated on the bottom of the timeline and floorplan view. Total covers for the shift and covers booked per 15-minute interlap. Tap to display party size breakdown booked for 15-minutes

  

---

  

### Review your floor plan in ResyOS

* On the Floor Plan view, you can check your seating capacities.
	+ The black circles are the minimum number of guests and the total circles are the maximum number of guests that can book on the table.

**Please note:**Any changes saved to the floor plan with automatically be reflected online.

  

---

  

### Review date closures including holidays and scheduled closed dates

1. In ResyOS, click on the calendar icon on the top right of the iPad.
2. Find the date you wish to close.
3. Hold down on the date.
4. Tap the close icon.

Or

1. In the Online Dashboard, click **Service** from the left-hand menu, then click **Calendar**.
2. Click on the date.
3. Select**Close shift or day.**
