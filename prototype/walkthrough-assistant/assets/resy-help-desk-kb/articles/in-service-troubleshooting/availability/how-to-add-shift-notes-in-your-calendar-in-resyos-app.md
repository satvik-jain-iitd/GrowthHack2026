# How do I add Shift Notes to my Calendar?

Add daily pop up notes in your iPad to alert your door team of private events, VIPs, local happenings, days off and more. Great for restaurants with lots of large parties and programming to keep everyone in the know.

## Adding a Note to a Day

1. On the top right corner of your screen tap the calendar icon.
2. Hold your finger down on the date you want to add a note.
3. Swipe up to "Add Note".
4. Select if you want the note for all day or a certain service period.
5. Type a title and/or description.
6. Toggle on "Prompt with Note" if you want to have a note pop-up when you toggle to that day.
7. Tap "Add Note to Day".

## Removing a Note

1. On the top right corner of your screen tap the calendar icon.
2. Find your note on the right-side panel.
3. Swipe left on the note to be removed.
4. Tap "Remove".

  
*Note: All notes and closed day notes are internal-facing only. A guest will see that you're closed for a day/service period, but they will not see why.*
