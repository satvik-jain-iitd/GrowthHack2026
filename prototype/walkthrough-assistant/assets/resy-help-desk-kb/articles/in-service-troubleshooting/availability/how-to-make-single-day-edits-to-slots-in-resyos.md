# Single Day Slots Updates in Resy OS

*Slot-based inventory is available for Platform 360 and Full Stack restaurant partners. If you are interested in learning more or upgrading, please reach out to your account manager or*[resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com)*. For a detailed walkthrough on how to set up slots through the Resy Dashboard, click*[here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-setup-slots-in-the-resy-dashboard-H1c3bvQ8d)*.*

**You can make quick single day edits to your slots on the iPad in Resy OS.**

**Tapping on a slot in Resy OS will show three options:**

* **Make In-house/Online** - This will adjust the slot to either in-house or online depending on the current status of the slot. The slot will only be adjusted for the single shift. To make recurring edits to your slot setup, visit the Resy Dashboard and select “Service” then “Shift Settings”
* **Unassign**- This will remove the slot from the current table assignment and place the slot in the “unassigned” section. The table will revert to its default attributes if a slot is removed (Online, In-house, or Walk-in). The slot will still be online or in-house whether it is assigned to a table or not
* **Edit -**This will allow you to edit all attributes of the slot including min/max party size, time, online status, and the ability to change the slot to a “manager slot”. The slot will only be adjusted for the single shift. To make recurring edits to your slot setup, visit the Resy Dashboard and select “Service” then “Shift Settings”

**Editing **slots using Single Day Edits****

When you choose the “Edit” option, the “Edit Slots” panel will open on the left-hand side of the screen. From there, you can:

* Change the start time. You can also do this by dragging and dropping the slot along the grid
* Change the table assignment. You can also do this by dragging and dropping the slot along the grid
* Change min party size
* Change max party size
* Change the availability setting to “Online” or “In-house”. You can also do this by tapping the slots and selecting “make in-house/online” from the menu
* Designate a slot as a “Manager Slot”. “Manager Slots” are another version of an in-house slot meant to be held back from standard in-house booking They’ll be clearly notated as “Manager Slot” on the timeline and booking model in Resy OS
* Apply an Exclusive Group to a slot. [View more about how to set up and use the Exclusive Groups feature](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-use-exclusive-online-booking-channels-BkihvQIO)
