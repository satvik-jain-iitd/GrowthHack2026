# Disable and Re-enable Online Bookings

Turn reservations off and back on for a specific shift or amount of time from your iPad through the 'Online Bookings' setting. 

**In this article:**

* [How to Disable Online Booking for an Entire Shift](#part1)
* [How to Enable Online Booking for an entire Shift](#part2)
* [How to Disable Online Booking for a Period of Time](#part3)
* [How to Re-Enable Online Booking for a Period of Time](#part4)

  

---

### 

## How to Disable Online Booking for an Entire Shift

1. Navigate to Reservation List under 'Booked'
2. Tap the word 'Online' next to 'Bookings'
3. Toggle OFF 'Bookable Online'
4. Tap 'Save'.Online Bookings will now be disabled and show as 'Offline' in Red.

[[IMAGE: how-to-turn-off-online-reservations-for-a-shift-or-period-of-time-in-resyos__img01]]

  

---

  
## How to Enable Online Booking for an Entire Shift

1. Navigate to Reservation List under 'Booked'
2. Tap the word 'Offline' next to 'Bookings'
3. Toggle ON 'Bookable Online'
4. Tap 'Save'. Online Bookings will now be enabled and show as 'Online' in Green.

[[IMAGE: how-to-turn-off-online-reservations-for-a-shift-or-period-of-time-in-resyos__img02]]

  

---

  

## How to Disable Online Booking for a Period of Time

1. Navigate to Reservation List under 'Booked'
2. Tap the word 'Online' next to 'Bookings'
3. Toggle OFF 'Bookable Online'
4. Toggle OFF 'Entire Shift'
5. Tap 'Start Time' and scroll to desired start time of blocked online reservations
6. Tap 'End Time' and scroll to desired end time
7. Tap 'Save' Online Bookings will now be disabled during the specific start and end time*.*

[[IMAGE: how-to-turn-off-online-reservations-for-a-shift-or-period-of-time-in-resyos__img03]]  

---

### 

## How to Re-Enable Online Booking for a Period of Time

1. Navigate to Reservation List under 'Booked'
2. Tap the word 'Offline' next to 'Bookings'
3. Toggle ON 'Bookable Online' to enable online booking for 'Entire Shift'
4. Tap 'Save'. Online Bookings will now be enabled for the entire shift period.

[[IMAGE: how-to-turn-off-online-reservations-for-a-shift-or-period-of-time-in-resyos__img04]]
