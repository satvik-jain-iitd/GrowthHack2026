# Booked List Icons

### **Notes Icon**

These icons are visual indicators to you and your team that there are notes or tags associated with this reservation.

[[IMAGE: what-are-the-icons-on-my-booked-list__img01]]

### **Yellow Note**

The Yellow Note indicates a guest request. When booking a reservation online, guests can add a special request or note to their reservation. Most guests use this space to let you know of any celebrations, seating preference, or dietary restrictions.

[[IMAGE: what-are-the-icons-on-my-booked-list__img02]]

### 

### **White Note**

The White Note indicates a visit note or a guest note. 

A visit note applies to that specific reservation only. For example, when making a reservation over the phone the guest may mention needing a high chair or ask to be seated at a specific table. These are visit notes and will add a white note icon to their reservation. 

A guest note applies to that guest and is shown on each of their visits. For example, when making a reservation over the phone the guest might come up in your guestbook and you’ll see a note that they prefer icy cold white wine. That guest note will add a white note icon to their reservation.

[[IMAGE: what-are-the-icons-on-my-booked-list__img03]]

### 

### **Blue Cake**

The Blue Cake indicates a special occasion. When adding a visit tag to your Resy OS setup, you have the option to create Occasion Visit Tags that light up in blue.

[[IMAGE: what-are-the-icons-on-my-booked-list__img04]]

  
**Orange Fork & Knife** 

The Orange Fork & Knife indicates an allergy or dietary restriction. When adding a guest tag to your Resy OS setup, you have the option to create “Allergy Visit” Tags that light up in orange.

[[IMAGE: what-are-the-icons-on-my-booked-list__img05]]

  
**To add an Allergy/Dietary Restriction guest tag:**

1. Tap your restaurant name in the top left-hand corner of the screen.
2. Tap “Settings”.
3. Tap “Tags”.
4. Select the “Guest” tab.
5. Tap “Add New”.
6. Enter the tag name.
7. Tap “Allergy”.
8. Tap “Create Tag”.

[Learn more about Guest and Visit Tags.](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/how-to-add-guest-and-visit-tags-to-a-reservation-in-resy-os-r14ZzPXL_)
