# View Guest Visit History in Resy OS

The “History” tab within the Guest Profile contains valuable information about all past reservations a guest has had at your restaurant. You’re able to see the following:

* Shift booked.
* Where your guest initiated their booking.
* Any changes made after the initial booking.
* Who initiated the change (whether it was in-house or done by the guest).

 

[[IMAGE: how-to-find-guest-history__img01]]

 

Above, we see all reservations the guest has at Burton Bistro. Tapping “View” will show you more information about the source of the reservation and any changes that have been made to the reservation, as seen below.

[[IMAGE: how-to-find-guest-history__img02]]

  

**Tip**: If you’re curious about the source of a reservation, just remember ResyOS = in-house booking, Resy = [resy.com](http://resy.com/), Resy widget = the booking widget on your website.
