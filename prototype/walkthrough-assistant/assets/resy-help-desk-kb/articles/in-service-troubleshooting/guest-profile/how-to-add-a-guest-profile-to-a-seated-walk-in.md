# How do I add a guest's profile information after seating a Walk-in?

Add a guest's name or phone number to a walk-in after they've been seated. Great for when you miss the VIP at the door but clock them at the table.

To add guest information to a walk-in party that has been seated, follow the instructions below.

1. Tap the walk-in profile on the seated list.
2. Tap the + icon to the right of the “Guest” field.
3. Search for the guest using their mobile number, name, or email and tap the profile to populate the guest details.
4. Tap "Add Guest to Walk-in" beneath the guest details.

You've successfully attributed this walk-in to your guest!

[[IMAGE: how-to-add-a-guest-profile-to-a-seated-walk-in__img01]]

***Please note****: If this is a new guest, nothing will populate for your search results and you'll see the option to tap “create new guest”.*
