# Resend a Booking Confirmation Email

You can resend a confirmation email to a guest anytime in Resy OS from the guest’s Reservation Profile.

1. Navigate to the date and shift of the reservation you are looking for.
2. Locate the reservation on the reservation list and tap on it to open the Reservation Profile window.
3. Tap the three dots icon to the far right of the guest’s name to open the Reservation Profile dropdown menu.
4. Select “'Resend Booking Email” to complete.
