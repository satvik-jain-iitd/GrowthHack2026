# Booking without Phone Number (Book on Behalf Of)

Learn how to **book a reservation without a guest's phone number**or book a reservation for a concierge or an assistant on behalf of someone else. Make sure to create a Resy profile for your restaurant with your restaurant's phone number. [Learn more about Concierge Tags and how to assign permissions](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/search?q=concierge+tags) for this feature. 

**You would use this feature in the following scenarios:**

* A concierge is booking on behalf of a guest. [Learn more about Concierge Tags](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/search?q=conerige+tag).
* You don't want to ask for a VIP's number.
* You forgot to ask for a number but have their name.

  

**In this article:**

* Add your Restaurant to your Guest Book
* How to Book on behalf of a Guest

  

---

  

## **Add your Restaurant to your Guest Book**

1. Click your restaurant name in the top-left corner.
2. Tap "Guest Book".
3. Tap "+" in the top-left corner of the guestbook.
4. Tap "Add" next to "Name" and add your restaurant's name.
5. Tap "Add" next to "Mobile" and add your restaurant's phone number.

**Please note**: This does not need to be a mobile number.

      6. Tap "Add Guest".

## **How to Book on Behalf of a Guest**

Now that you've added your restaurant profile to the guestbook, you're ready to start using the “Book on Behalf of” feature:

1. Open the Booking Module.
2. Select the date, party size, time, and table type by scrolling on each column.
3. On the right-hand side, enter your restaurant's name.
4. Select the restaurant profile.
5. Tap the three vertical dots to the right of the restaurant name.
6. Select “**Book on Behalf of**”.
7. Type the guest's name that you'd like to appear on the reservation.
8. Tap “Add Guest” and add any notes or tags that you'd like.
9. Tap “Reserve.” The name on the reservation will be the actual guest's name and the “Reservation Contact” will be the restaurant profile.
