# Seat a Guest in Resy OS

Seat a party by changing their status to “Seated” from the reservation list or the table on your iPad.

### **Option 1: Seating a Guest from the Booked List**

1. Tap on the status icon to the left of the guest's name that you want to seat.
2. Change the status to “Seated”.

[[IMAGE: how-to-seat-a-guest-in-resyos__img01]]  

### **Option 2: Seating a Guest from a Table**

1. Tap on the table that the guest you want to seat is plotted on.
2. To the left of their name, tap the status icon.
3. Change status to “Seated”.

[[IMAGE: how-to-seat-a-guest-in-resyos__img02]]  

Once a guest is seated, the reservation will move automatically to the "Seated" list on the left-hand side below your booked list.
