# Update Waitlist and Reservation Status

Update the status on reservations and waitlist parties from the reservation list or table directly. Tap on the table or the guest's name, then tap the colorful circle to open the status menu and select!

## 

## Updating Waitlist Statuses

  

1. Tap the status icon to the left of the guest’s name.
2. Tap the new status.

[[IMAGE: how-to-update-a-reservation-status__img01]]**Please note:** Changing a waitlisted guest status to any of the Reservation statuses will automatically move the guest to the Booked list.

[[IMAGE: how-to-update-a-reservation-status__img02]]  

## Updating Reservation Statuses

**Option 1**: Bookings Panel

1. Tap the status icon to the left of the guest’s name on the “Booked” or “Seated” list.
2. Tap the new status.

[[IMAGE: how-to-update-a-reservation-status__img03]]## 

**Option 2**: Change the status from the table

1. Tap on the table.
2. Tap the status icon to the left of the name of the reservation.
3. Tap the new status.

[[IMAGE: how-to-update-a-reservation-status__img04]]**Please note:** If you would like to add a new status or customize the color of an existing status, please contact support at [support@resy.com](mailto:support@resy.com).
