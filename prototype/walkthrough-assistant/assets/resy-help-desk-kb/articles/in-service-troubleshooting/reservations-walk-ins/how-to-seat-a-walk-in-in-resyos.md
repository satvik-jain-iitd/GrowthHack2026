# Seat a Walk-in in Resy OS

Seat walk-in guests directly on the table, with or without a phone number! You can always go back and add in the guest's name later.

**Please note**: You can only seat walk-in guests during the current service period. Phone numbers are not required to book a walk-in, but you can add one if you want to keep your guest book up to date. 

**Seating a Walk-In**

1. Select “Floor Plan View”.
2. Hold down with your finger on the table that you want to seat a walk-in on.
3. Slide finger to right or tap “>” to seat a walk-in.
4. Change the guest count.
5. Add a phone number (optional).
6. Tap "Seat Walk-In".

[[IMAGE: how-to-seat-a-walk-in-in-resyos__img01]]  

**Please note**: Once you seat a walk-in, they will automatically show up under your seated list.
