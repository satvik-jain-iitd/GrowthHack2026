# Quick Replies Feature

Save frequently used text messages to quickly communicate with guests on your Notify, Booked, and Waitlists from your iPad. You can use this feature to remind guests of local health mandates, allergy reminders, and anything else they need to know.

**Email text may not include any content that can be considered promotional such as offers or other marketing messages.**

 

**Please note:** Quick Replies are separate and different from your automatic text messages sent through Resy. Quick Replies allow you to save common text messages you send your guests on the Notify, Waitlist, and Booked lists. To learn more about the automatic messages, please read [this](https://helpdesk.resy.com/en_us/standard-resy-automated-sms-content-S110vQIu) article.

  

---

  

### **How to Add and Edit Quick Replies**

Quick Replies are sent **manually**and are **device-specific**, meaning these are sent by your team and unique to each iPad or iPhone. 

To add Quick Replies on the iPad:

1. Tap your restaurant’s name in the top-left corner of the screen then select Settings > Quick Replies.
2. Fill in each blank field with your Quick Reply for the corresponding list (Waitlist, Reservation, or Notify). Please keep in mind there is a recommended 160-character limit on each SMS, including spaces.
3. Once the message is written, tap the "x" in the top-right corner and your messages will save automatically.

### Examples of Quick Replies

* **Waitlist**– This text is sent to guests on your waitlist.
	+ “Please note we only seat complete parties. Thank you for your patience!”
* **Reservation**– This text is sent to guests on your booked list. You can use this to ask guests about dietary restrictions and celebrations.
	+ **Tip**: You’ll want to introduce yourself, so make sure to use your restaurant’s name!“Hi from Tom’s Restaurant! Please let us know if you’re celebrating or have any dietary restrictions. Looking forward to having you in!”
* **Notify**– This text is sent to guests on your notify list. You can reach out to them letting them know you also accept walk-ins, your bar is first-come-first-served, or to give you a call!
	+ “So glad you want to join us at Tom’s Restaurant! Please give us a call at 123-456-7890 and we’ll do our best to squeeze you in!”

### How to Send Quick Replies

Send Quick Replies directly from the reservation on your iPad or iPhone. 

1. Tap into the ‘Reservation’, ‘Waitlist’, or ‘Notify’ entry and tap on the “Chat” tab.[[IMAGE: text-message-quick-replies__img02]]
2. Tap the blue chat bubble with a lightning bolt in the bottom-right corner of the window to populate your Quick Reply.
3. Tap “Send”.
4. Tap on the next name from your reservation list, tap the chat bubble, tap “Send”, and repeat to send to all your guests!
