# How far away can guests be from my restaurant when adding themselves to my Mobile Waitlist?

The Mobile Waitlist feature allows guests to add themselves to your waitlist remotely through the Resy App. The option to join a restaurant's mobile waitlist is limited to guests in close proximity to the restaurant. To determine your Mobile Waitlist radius please see the list of Resy locations below.

 

**Locations and Mobile Waitlist Radius**

* **All Locations Not Specified** - 5 miles
* **Atlanta** - 5 miles
* **Austin** - 5 miles
* **Boston** - 2 miles
* **Charleston** - 5 miles
* **Chicago** - 5 miles
* **DC** - 2 miles
* **Denver** - 5 miles
* **Houston** - 5 miles
* **LA** - 5 miles
* **Miami** - 5 miles
* **Nashville** - 5 miles
* **NYC** - 1 mile
* **Philadelphia** - 1 mile
* **Portland** - 5 miles
* **San Francisco** - 3 miles
* **Seattle** - 5 miles

For more information about the Mobile Waitlist feature, please visit [Resy's Mobile Waitlist Feature](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-use-mobile-waitlist-rkj_bD7Iu).
