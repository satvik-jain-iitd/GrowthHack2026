# Using Prepay in ResyOS

Use PrePay when someone calls to buy their friend a bottle of wine or cover the whole meal for one of the guests in your restaurant. PrePay sends them a secure payment link via text to pay and the funds are deposited in your account. No more credit card authorization forms!

**Please Note:**

* PrePay is an exclusive feature for our 360 partners. If you're interested in upgrading, please reach out to our Support Team via chat or [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com).
* Prior to using the feature, ensure your [banking information](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/how-to-edit-your-venue-settings-rkTjbwm8O) is on file in the Dashboard. We won't be able to disburse the funds to you without it.

## **How to Add a PrePay**

1. Tap on the reservation from the booked list.
2. Tap on the three dots on the upper right.
3. Tap "**Add Prepay**".

## **How to Add the Payer and Payment**

1. Enter the mobile number of the Payer *(They will receive a link via SMS).*
2. Choose the Payer from the Resy database or create a New Guest.
3. Add in an Item Description *(Bottle of Wine, Full Dinner, Champagne Toast, etc*).
4. Add in the Amount the payer has agreed to pay.
5. Note whether it's a surprise (optional).
6. Once all information is filled out, tap "Send PrePay Request".

| **Please Note**: The gratuity percentages are there for the payer to select, the prepay feature doesn't calculate this prior to charging, so you will have to calculate the total charge. The final price charged should always include both the sales tax and the gratuity. |

## **Prepay Statuses**

**Pending**: This status means a Prepay request has been sent to the Payer, and they need to tap the link in the text message they received to enter the payment details.

**Not Charged:**This status means the Payer has filled out the payment details but they haven't been charged for the Prepay yet.

[[IMAGE: how-to-use-resy-prepay-feature-in-resyos__img03]]**Charged:**This status means you have clicked 'Charge' on the guests Prepay to charge the Payers credit card.

## **How to Charge PrePay Requests**

**Please Note:** If the status is still "Pending" before service, call the payer to ensure that they can add their credit card information, or the PrePay cannot be charged.

* Before the guest leaves the restaurant and the check is closed in the POS, tap the Charge button and enter in the final amount of the item or meal. **Note: You should factor in both sales tax and the gratuity into the final cost*
* Once charged through Resy the status will change from "Not Charged" to "Charged".   
  
****If the status does not change to "Charged" and stays as "Not Charged", there is an error in the payer's credit card information and they should be contacted to gather proper payment****

The payer will get a receipt via text message when the payment is processed successfully.

  
  

## **How to Manage a Failed PrePay Attempt**

**If the guest is subscribed to SMS, you'll be presented with a "Request New Card" option.**

1. Click “Request New Card,” and an SMS link will be sent to the guest.
2. Click “Charge”. This will be enabled again once the guest adds a new card via the link.
3. Complete the PrePay payment flow as usual.

[[IMAGE: how-to-use-resy-prepay-feature-in-resyos__img04]][[IMAGE: how-to-use-resy-prepay-feature-in-resyos__img05]]  

**If the guest is unsubscribed to SMS, you’ll first need to instruct the guest to text “Start” to 346637.**

•    You’ll no longer be able to select a different card on the guests’ behalf.

•    A "Guest opt-in to receiving new payment link is pending” message will show under PREPAY. 

1. Click “Request New Card” to send a new payment link once the user has opted in to SMS.
2. Click "Charge". This is enabled again once a new payment is added.
3. Complete the Prepay payment flow as usual.

[[IMAGE: how-to-use-resy-prepay-feature-in-resyos__img06]]

  

## **How to Reconcile PrePay Charges on your POS**

* The item or meal should be charged to a house account on the POS systems. You can create a Resy house account button on your POS for organization sake, and **again ensure that sales tax and gratuity are factored in to the PrePay amounts.**
* Payments are disbursed via direct deposit 48-72 hours after the payer's credit card is charged.
