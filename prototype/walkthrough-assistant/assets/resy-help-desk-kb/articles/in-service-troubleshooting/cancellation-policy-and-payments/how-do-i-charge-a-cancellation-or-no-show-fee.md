# Cancellation Fee Charges

#### Once a guest violates the cancellation or no show policy set-up, you will decide whether or not to charge them. If you decide to charge, you can follow these steps to manually charge the credit card on file. **This is not an automated process.**Disbursement of these fees to the restaurant's bank account is set up in Stripe.

***Please Note:*** Only those users with the permission to 'Charge cancel fee' will be able to make the charge. Read more about giving users permissions [here](https://resy.kustomer.help/en_us/user-permissions-r1zCWDmU_).

#### **In this article:**

* How do I know if I can charge a guest?
* How do I charge the fee?

---

### 

### **How do I know if I can charge a guest?**

If a cancellation or no-show reservation from a guest falls within the cancellation policy, a little icon with a "!" in a circle will appear below their name on the "Xcl/No Show" List.

  
[[IMAGE: how-do-i-charge-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee__img01]]

---

### 

### **How do I Charge the Fee?**

1) On the left side of the Resy OS app, tap on the reservation from the**"Xcl/No Show"** list that is eligible to be charged (Marked by the exclamation point icon - above)

2) Tap the icon with an "!" located on the top bar in the reservation profile screen

[[IMAGE: how-do-i-charge-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee__img02]]

3) Select your name from the accountability screen and tap "Charge"

[[IMAGE: how-do-i-charge-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee__img03]]

4) Cancellation fee funds will be disbursed directly to your bank account on file.
