# Contact Resy Support

We're here to help. If you're a diner seeking support, please visit our About & Help page: [https://resy.com/about-and-help](https://resy.com/about-and-help "https://resy.com/about-and-help").

For restaurants support, our Support Team can be reached via Email at [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com) or Live Chat on the ResyOS App and Dashboard 7 days a week from 8am to 2am EST.

**In this article:**

* Contact Support for Diners
* Contact Support for Restaurants

## **For Diners**

For assistance with managing Reservations, the Resy App, and Resy.com, please visit our About & Help page: [https://resy.com/about-and-help](https://resy.com/about-and-help "https://resy.com/about-and-help").

## 

## **For Restaurants**

To contact Resy support, select one of the following options for assistance with the ResyOS App, ResyOS Dashboard, and Restaurant inquiries.

1) Browse the [Resy Helpdesk](http://helpdesk.resy.com) for basic Training and How-Tos. 

2) Live Chat: Chat directly from the ResyOS App by the Support Chat button in the top-left-hand corner, next to the restaurant name, in the iPad view.

  
[[IMAGE: how-to-contact-resy-support__img01]]

  

  

3) Chat directly from the ResyOS Dashboard by tapping the Chat icon in bottom left hand corner of webpage. 

4) Email [resysupport@resy.com](mailto:resysupport@resy.com).
