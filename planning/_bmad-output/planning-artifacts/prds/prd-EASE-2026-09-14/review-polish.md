**Purpose / audience read:** This document exists to help the PM, Team EASE, and downstream workflow owners align on exactly what the GrowthHack 2026 MVP will build, prove, and hand off.

**Chosen structure model:** Strategic/Context (Pyramid)

**Reader calibration:** Humans

**Style / voice to preserve**
- Assertive PRD voice with explicit decisions and strong verbs
- Journey-first framing that helps readers picture the demo before reading the FR stack
- Bold lead terms and recurring labels (`Description`, `Functional Requirements`, `Consequences`, `Out of Scope`) that make the document scannable
- Signature phrases that carry the product thesis, especially the **Save-vs-Activate Gap** and the **Ask → Guide → Complete → Observe → Learn → Approve → Improve** loop
