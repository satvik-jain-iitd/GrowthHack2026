# Brief-to-PRD Reconciliation

## Gaps found
1. **Single-Day Edit flow is in the brief's scope, but the PRD demotes it to optional/out-of-scope.**
   - Brief scope says the hero journey should include the 7-step flow and specifically says to "handle one-off single-day edits." The PRD's §6.2 makes Single Day Edit explicitly not required for MVP.

2. **The brief's judging-strategy emphasis on business-partner presence is not translated into PRD requirements/success metrics.**
   - The brief says the 30% Business Unit Value criterion should be visibly satisfied by having Meagan or Rachel in the pitch narrative/on camera, not just by product behavior. The PRD covers the specialist review flow, but does not require or measure this business-value proof point.

3. **The brief's Innovation framing is broader than the PRD's FRs.**
   - The brief explicitly says EASE should make the "previously unknown improvement opportunity" and friction reduction obvious to judges. The PRD centers the save-vs-activate gap, but does not separately encode the need to *signal* the "no in-product guidance exists today" contrast as a demo requirement or success metric.

4. **The brief's Hack Completeness weighting implication is not carried forward as an explicit scope strategy.**
   - The brief says Completeness is only 10% and that the team should prioritize feasibility/innovation/business-value over polish or breadth. The PRD has scope cuts, but it doesn't explicitly preserve that judge-weighted tradeoff as a planning principle.
